  # <Color id="dark_aqua">Ender IO</Color>

  <ItemImage id="enderio:yeta_wrench" scale="2" />

  Ender IO is a tech mod focused on machines, conduits, and alloys. Smelt special alloys in the Alloy Smelter, crush ores in the SAG Mill, run power through Capacitors, and connect everything with compact multi-type Conduits that share a single block space.
</Column>

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Color id="red">Unlocked at Jotunheim stage.</Color>

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  ## <Color id="gold">General Progression</Color>
</Column>

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.5"/>

1. Obtain <ItemLink id="enderio:grains_of_infinity" /> by setting fire to Bedrock or Deepslate
2. Craft a <ItemLink id="enderio:stirling_generator" /> for early power
3. Smelt <ItemLink id="enderio:dark_steel_ingot" /> — the foundation of most EnderIO recipes
4. Build an <ItemLink id="enderio:alloy_smelter" /> and <ItemLink id="enderio:sag_mill" /> for processing
5. Upgrade machines with <ItemLink id="enderio:basic_capacitor" /> through <ItemLink id="enderio:octadic_capacitor" />
6. Craft <ItemLink id="enderio:energetic_alloy_ingot" /> and <ItemLink id="enderio:vibrant_alloy_ingot" /> for advanced recipes
7. Set up <Color id="dark_aqua">Conduits</Color> for compact item, fluid, and energy transport
8. Explore soul machinery with <ItemLink id="enderio:soularium_ingot" /> for Powered Spawners
