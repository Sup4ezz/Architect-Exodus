# <Color id="dark_aqua">Other Machines</Color>

<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  # <Color id="dark_aqua">Other Machines</Color>
  <ItemImage id="enderio:enchanter" scale="2" />
  Beyond the core processing and soul machines, Ender IO offers a range of utility machines for experience, crafting automation, and fluid storage.
</Column>

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Color id="gold">All machines accept Capacitor upgrades</Color> to increase speed, energy storage, and efficiency. See [Power](power.md) for capacitor details.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

***

<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  ## <Color id="gold">Experience</Color>
</Column>

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="enderio:enchanter" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Enchanter</Color>
</Row>

Crafts enchanted books using raw materials and experience instead of an Enchantment Table. Place the appropriate item and a Book & Quill on the input slots and supply power. Different materials produce different enchantments — check JEI for the full list of available enchantments.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="enderio:xp_obelisk" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">XP Obelisk</Color>
</Row>

One of the best ways to store experience, especially when combined with the <ItemLink id="enderio:xp_vacuum" />. Allows you to store and withdraw levels from its tank — one level at a time, ten at a time, or all at once.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

***

<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  ## <Color id="gold">Utility Machines</Color>
</Column>

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="enderio:painting_machine" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Painting Machine</Color>
</Row>

Paints blocks to look like other blocks. Place the block you want to disguise and the block whose appearance you want to copy. Works with Conduit Facades, Pressure Plates, Fences, and more. Great for aesthetic builds.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="enderio:crafter" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Crafter</Color>
</Row>

An autocrafting machine that repeatedly crafts a set recipe using items from adjacent inventories. Set the recipe in the GUI and supply materials.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="enderio:impulse_hopper" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Impulse Hopper</Color>
</Row>

A smart hopper that transfers items with redstone control. Can be configured to move specific quantities per pulse, making it useful for precise item distribution in automation setups.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

***

<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  ## <Color id="gold">Fluid Storage</Color>
</Column>

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="enderio:fluid_tank" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Fluid Tank</Color>
</Row>

A basic fluid storage block. Stores a moderate amount of any fluid. Can be filled and drained via Fluid Conduits or by right-clicking with a bucket.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="enderio:pressurized_fluid_tank" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Pressurized Fluid Tank</Color>
</Row>

An upgraded fluid tank with significantly more capacity. Crafted with Dark Steel components for higher-volume fluid storage needs.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="enderio:drain" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Drain</Color>
</Row>

Collects fluids from the world when placed next to a fluid source block. Connect it to a Fluid Conduit or tank to automatically gather water, lava, or other fluids.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>
