<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  ## <Color id="gold">Основные сплавы</Color>
</Column>

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

Ender IO revolves around special alloys. Each is crafted in the Alloy Smelter by combining base metals with other ingredients. Here are the alloys you will encounter in progression order.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="enderio:dark_steel_ingot" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Dark Steel</Color>
</Row>

The most important Ender IO alloy. Used in nearly every machine, tool, and armor piece. Crafted from Iron, Coal, and Obsidian in the Alloy Smelter. Dark Steel is your first milestone — once you have it, the rest of EnderIO opens up.


<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="enderio:copper_alloy_ingot" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Copper Alloy</Color>
</Row>

An early-game alloy made from Copper and Silicon. Used in basic machine components and conduit crafting.


<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="enderio:redstone_alloy_ingot" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Redstone Alloy</Color>
</Row>

Made from Redstone and Silicon. Used in redstone-related conduits and components.


<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="enderio:conductive_alloy_ingot" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Conductive Alloy</Color>
</Row>

Combines Iron, Redstone, and Copper Alloy. Used in energy conduits and basic power infrastructure.


<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="enderio:pulsating_alloy_ingot" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Pulsating Alloy</Color>
</Row>

Made from Iron and Ender Pearl. Used in ender-themed items and advanced conduits.


<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="enderio:energetic_alloy_ingot" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Energetic Alloy</Color>
</Row>

A mid-tier alloy made from Gold, Redstone, and Glowstone. Key ingredient for Double Layer Capacitors and Vibrant Alloy.


<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="enderio:vibrant_alloy_ingot" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Vibrant Alloy</Color>
</Row>

The high-tier alloy combining Energetic Alloy and Ender Pearl. Used in Octadic Capacitors, the Electromagnet, and endgame recipes.


<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="enderio:soularium_ingot" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Soularium</Color>
</Row>

A soul-infused alloy made from Gold and Soul Sand. Required for the Slice'N'Splice, Soul Binder, and Powered Spawner — the late-game soul machinery line.


<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="enderio:end_steel_ingot" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">End Steel</Color>
</Row>

An endgame alloy combining Dark Steel, Obsidian, and End Stone. Used in the most advanced EnderIO equipment.


<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

  ## <Color id="gold">Glass Types</Color>
</Column>

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="enderio:fused_quartz" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Fused Quartz</Color>
</Row>

Made by smelting 4 Nether Quartz in the Alloy Smelter. A transparent block used in various recipes. Can be dyed in 16 colors.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="enderio:clear_glass" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Clear Glass</Color>
</Row>

A completely transparent glass block. Also available in 16 dyed variants. Used in conduit facades and decorative builds.
