<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  ## <Color id="gold">Essential Tools</Color>
</Column>

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>


<Row>
  <ItemImage id="dark_steel_sword" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">The Ender</Color>
</Row>

A diamond-tier sword made of Dark Steel. It is great to use for gathering Mob Heads.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="enderio:yeta_wrench" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Yeta Wrench</Color>
</Row>

The Ender IO multitool. Right-click machines to rotate them. Shift-right-click to instantly pick up machines while preserving their settings. Essential for working with conduits — right-click a conduit connection to open its GUI, or shift-right-click to disconnect a conduit from a specific side.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="enderio:electromagnet" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Electromagnet</Color>
</Row>

When activated (right-click to toggle), pulls nearby dropped items toward you from up to 5 blocks away. Useful in mob farms and mining operations. Requires <ItemLink id="enderio:vibrant_alloy_ingot" /> to craft — a late-game convenience item.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="enderio:experience_rod" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Experience Rod</Color>
</Row>

Stores and retrieves XP. Right-click to store your experience levels into the rod; shift-right-click to withdraw them. Handy for banking XP before dangerous situations.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  ## <Color id="gold">Travel</Color>
</Column>

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="enderio:staff_of_travelling" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Staff of Travelling</Color>
</Row>

A powerful tool that allows you to teleport forward a short distance by shift-right-clicking. You can also use this mechanic to pass through thin walls! While holding the staff, nearby <ItemLink id="enderio:travel_anchor" /> blocks are visible through walls.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="enderio:travel_anchor" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Travel Anchor</Color>
</Row>

A waypoint block that can be teleported to when holding the <ItemLink id="enderio:staff_of_travelling" />, as long as the anchor is within visible range. Place these around your base to create a quick teleportation network.
