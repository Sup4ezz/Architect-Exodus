# <Color id="aqua">Charged Certus Quartz</Color>

<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  # <Color id="aqua">Charged Certus Quartz</Color>
  <ItemImage id="ae2:charged_certus_quartz_crystal" scale="2" />

  Charged certus quartz drives Fluix recipes and several mid-tier AE2 components. The vanilla AE2 <Color id="red">Charger has been removed</Color> from this pack — you charge certus another way.
</Column>

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

***

<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  ## <Color id="gold">Primary Method — Powah Energizing Orb</Color>
</Column>

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="powah:energizing_orb" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Energizing Orb</Color>
</Row>

Drop a stack of <ItemLink id="ae2:certus_quartz_crystal" /> onto the pedestal under a <ItemLink id="powah:energizing_orb" /> and supply <Color id="green">10,000 FE</Color> from Powah Energizing Rods. Each crystal energizes into a <ItemLink id="ae2:charged_certus_quartz_crystal" />.

See the [Powah guide](powah:guide:energizing_orb.md) for orb setup, rod placement, and the early-game options for getting power into the orb (Furnator + Coal works fine as a starter).

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

***

<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  ## <Color id="gold">Alternative — Lychee Lightning Channeling</Color>
</Column>

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

If you have not built an Energizing Orb yet, you can also charge certus the old-fashioned way: place a <ItemLink id="ae2:certus_quartz_crystal" /> on the ground and let lightning strike it — a Lychee channeling recipe converts it into Charged Certus directly. A <Color id="green">Lightning Rod</Color> or a thunderstorm-aiming setup makes this consistent.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>
