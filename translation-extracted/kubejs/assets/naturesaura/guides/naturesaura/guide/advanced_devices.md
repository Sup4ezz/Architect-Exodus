<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  ## <Color id="gold">Advanced Aura Generators</Color>
</Column>

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

These generators produce significantly more aura than the starter options but require more advanced materials:

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="firework_generator" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Firework Generator</Color>
</Row>

Generates aura from firework explosions. More elaborate fireworks produce more aura.


<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="potion_generator" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Potion Generator (Ritual of the Brewer)</Color>
</Row>

Generates aura by consuming potions. Requires a Nether Brick structure (28 Nether Bricks and 12 Red Nether Bricks).


<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="chorus_generator" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Chorus Generator</Color>
</Row>

Generates aura using Chorus Fruit, tapping into End-related energies.


<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

***

<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  ## <Color id="gold">Generator Modifiers</Color>
</Column>

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="generator_limit_remover" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Generator Limit Remover</Color>
</Row>

Removes the built-in production cap on nearby generators, allowing them to produce aura without limit. Use with caution.


<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="lower_limiter" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Lower Limiter</Color>
</Row>

Limits how low aura can drop in the area, providing a safety floor for aura levels.


<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

***

<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  ## <Color id="gold">World Manipulation</Color>
</Column>

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="weather_changer" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Weather Changer</Color>
</Row>

Uses aura to change the weather. Consumes a significant amount of aura per use.


<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="time_changer" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Time Changer</Color>
</Row>

Manipulates the time of day using aura. Requires a <Color id="green">Clock Hand</Color> to operate.


<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="spawn_lamp" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Spawn Lamp</Color>
</Row>

Prevents hostile mob spawning in a wide area around it, consuming aura to maintain the effect.


<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="spring" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Spring</Color>
</Row>

Creates a water source that uses aura to function.


<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

***

<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  ## <Color id="gold">Blast Furnace Booster</Color>
</Column>

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="blast_furnace_booster" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Blast Furnace Booster</Color>
</Row>

The Blast Furnace Booster enhances the smelting speed of a Blast Furnace by feeding it aura energy.


<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>
