  ## <Color id="gold">Botanist Gear (Infused Iron)</Color>
</Column>

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="infused_iron_pickaxe" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Botanist Gear</Color>
</Row>

Infused Iron equipment matches mundane iron in durability and strength, but can be repaired by sneaking while wearing an <ItemLink id="aura_cache" /> containing aura.

<ItemGrid>
  <ItemIcon id="infused_iron_pickaxe" />
  <ItemIcon id="infused_iron_helmet" />
</ItemGrid>

<Color id="yellow">Set bonus:</Color> Wearing the full armor set applies a short Withering effect to any attackers.

The tools also have small unique utilities -- for example, the Botanist Pickaxe can grow moss on certain stone blocks.
