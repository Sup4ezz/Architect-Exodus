  ## <Color id="gold">Brilliant Fiber</Color>
</Column>

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="gold_fiber" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Brilliant Fiber</Color>
</Row>

Brilliant Fiber — первый предмет, который нужно создать. Используйте его на листве дерева и дождитесь, пока энергия распространится по кроне. Когда листья полностью позолотятся, соберите Gold Leaf.

<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  ## <Color id="gold">Gold Leaf</Color>
</Column>

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="gold_leaf" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Gold Leaf</Color>
</Row>

Gold Leaf — основной ингредиент для создания предметов Nature's Aura. Собирайте его с листвы, полностью преобразованной Brilliant Fiber. По мере развития понадобится много таких листьев.

  ## <Color id="gold">Gold Powder</Color>
</Column>

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="gold_powder" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Gold Powder</Color>
</Row>

Gold Powder is crafted from Gold Leaf and is essential for the Ritual of the Forest. You will need a ring of 16 Gold Powder placed around a sapling each time you perform the ritual.

<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  ## <Color id="gold">Bottled Aura</Color>
</Column>

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="bottle_two_the_rebottling" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Bottled Aura</Color>
</Row>

Aura saturates the air in different realms. By crafting a Bottle and Two and using it in various dimensions, you can collect different types of bottled aura.


There are several types of bottled aura. The bottle you produce depends on the <Color id="green">aura type</Color> of the realm you bottle in:

- <Color id="yellow">Bottled Sunlight</Color> -- collected in overworld-type realms: <Color id="green">Niflheim, Midgard, Asgard, the Overworld, and Hyperboxes</Color>
- <Color id="dark_purple">Bottled Darkness</Color> -- collected in nether-type realms: <Color id="green">Muspelheim, Nidhogg's domain, the Blood Magic Dungeon, and the vanilla Nether</Color>
- <Color id="gray">Bottled Ghosts</Color> -- collected in end-type realms: <Color id="green">Jotunheim, Bifrost, Alfheim, and the vanilla End</Color>
- <Color id="dark_gray">Bottled Vacuum</Color> -- obtained in regions where local aura is severely low, regardless of dimension
- <Color id="aqua">Bottled Substance</Color> -- holds a vague, uninfluenced aura with no particular known use

  ### <Color id="aqua">Environmental Eye</Color>

A critical tool for working with aura. The Environmental Eye allows you to see the general levels of aura at your location, as well as how much aura is contained in blocks that can hold it. Craft one early and keep it handy.
