<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  ## <Color id="gold">How It Works</Color>
</Column>

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

The growth of a sapling into a tree focuses a large amount of natural energy. The Ritual of the Forest redirects this energy into the creation of magical items and artifacts.

To perform the ritual:

1. Place up to <Color id="yellow">8 Wood Stands</Color> in a ring around a central sapling
2. Surround the sapling with a ring of <Color id="yellow">16 Gold Powder</Color>
3. Place the required recipe ingredients on the Wood Stands
4. The sapling will grow and the items will be transformed

> <Color id="red">Important:</Color> The Gold Powder ring is consumed with each ritual and must be replaced. Not all 8 pedestals need to be present, and the order of ingredients does not matter.

<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  ## <Color id="gold">Sapling Types</Color>
</Column>

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

The most widespread tree in the realms is oak, so <ItemLink id="minecraft:oak_sapling" /> is the sapling required for most Ritual of the Forest recipes. A few special recipes require <ItemLink id="minecraft:jungle_sapling" /> instead. Check the Book of Natural Aura or JEI for specific recipe requirements.
