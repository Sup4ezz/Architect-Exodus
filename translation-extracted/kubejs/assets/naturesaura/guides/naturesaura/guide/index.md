  # <Color id="green">Nature's Aura</Color>

  <ItemImage id="gold_leaf" scale="2" />

  Nature's Aura is a mod themed around the manipulation and creation of Aura: an energy spread through the essence of the worlds. Different realms carry different variants of aura, but they are mostly compatible. Harness this natural energy to craft powerful items, generate resources, and bend the world to your will.
</Column>

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  ## <Color id="gold">Progression Overview</Color>
</Column>

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

1. Craft <ItemLink id="gold_fiber" /> and use it on tree leaves to grow <ItemLink id="gold_leaf" />
2. Build the <ItemLink id="wood_stand" /> pedestals and perform the Ritual of the Forest
3. Construct a <ItemLink id="nature_altar" /> for aura-infused crafting
4. Plant <ItemLink id="ancient_sapling" /> to maintain local aura levels
5. Set up Aura Generators to push aura beyond normal levels
6. Build an <ItemLink id="offering_table" /> to trade with the gods for powerful materials
7. Craft advanced tools, armor, and automation devices
