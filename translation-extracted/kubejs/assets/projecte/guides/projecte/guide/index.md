  # <Color id="dark_purple">ProjectE</Color>

  <ItemImage id="red_matter" scale="2" />

  ProjectE is an alchemy and equivalent exchange mod. Every item has an EMC (Energy-Matter Covalence) value. Learn items by placing them in a Transmutation Table, then convert between items of equal EMC value. Generate EMC passively with Energy Collectors, store it in Klein Stars, and condense items automatically with Energy Condensers. Craft powerful Dark Matter and Red Matter tools, armor, and magical rings.
