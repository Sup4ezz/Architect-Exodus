# <Color id="dark_purple">Changes in This Pack</Color>

<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  # <Color id="dark_purple">Changes in This Pack</Color>
  <ItemImage id="transmutation_table" scale="2" />

  ProjectE is heavily reworked in Architect's Exodus. Left alone it would let you skip most of the other mods, so its recipes, its EMC table and its gear list have all been rebuilt around the realm progression.

  Read this page before you plan an EMC setup. A lot of what you will find in older guides and videos does not apply here.
</Column>

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

***

<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  ## <Color id="gold">Removed Equipment</Color>
</Column>

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Color id="red">All Dark Matter and Red Matter tools, weapons and armour are gone.</Color> Their recipes are removed and the items are hidden from JEI. That covers every sword, pickaxe, axe, shovel, hoe, shears and hammer at both tiers, both full armour sets, the Katar and the Morning Star, and the Gem armour set.

The <Color id="aqua">Dark Matter and Red Matter Furnaces are removed as well</Color>, so do not plan around them for smelting.

Dark Matter and Red Matter themselves still exist. They are crafting materials for machines and for the Project Expansion tiers, nothing more.

<Color id="green">Rings, amulets and utility items are untouched</Color>, so the accessory line is the reason to invest in ProjectE here. See [Rings & Amulets](rings.md).

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

***

<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  ## <Color id="gold">The EMC Table Is Rebalanced</Color>
</Column>

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

Several hundred items have had their EMC values overridden. Two things to understand:

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

### <Color id="aqua">Many items have no EMC at all</Color>

An item with zero EMC cannot be learned, transmuted, condensed or burned for value. Broad categories are switched off:

* <Color id="aqua">Common renewables</Color> - planks, stripped logs and wood, leaves, crops, mushrooms
* <Color id="aqua">Consumables</Color> - every potion and tipped arrow, golden apples, dragon breath, chorus fruit, phantom membrane
* <Color id="aqua">Trophies</Color> - all mob heads, nether star, dragon egg, end crystal, music discs
* <Color id="aqua">Other mods' progression materials</Color> - Mystical Agriculture essences and prosperity shards, Botania manasteel, elementium, terrasteel, gaia and every rune, MythicBotany runes, Blood Magic will crystals and hellforged, Ars Nouveau essences, Tinkers' alloys, Draconic chaos items and the Dragon Heart, Ice and Fire drops, Bountiful Hearts items

That last group is the important one. <Color id="yellow">EMC will not shortcut another mod's progression.</Color> If a material is the reward for doing a different mod's questline, it has been taken out of the system.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

### <Color id="aqua">Values have been flattened and re-tiered</Color>

Where items do keep EMC, the numbers have been reworked so the ore chain is the sensible path rather than a food farm. A sample:

| Item | EMC |
|---|---|
| Stone types, deepslate, tuff | 1 |
| Gravel, flint, blackstone | 4 |
| Any crop or simple food | 16 |
| Copper, zinc, aluminium ingot | 126 |
| Iron, tin ingot | 252 |
| Lead, silver, iesnium ingot | 504 |
| Nickel ingot | 1,017 |
| Ender pearl | 1,024 |
| Diamond | 8,190 |
| Cobalt ingot | 6,138 |
| Netherite scrap | 12,285 |

<Color id="gold">Tip:</Color> if you are unsure whether something has EMC, check it in JEI before building a farm around it.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  ## <Color id="gold">Rebuilt Recipes</Color>
</Column>

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

Most of the early ProjectE recipes are replaced so that entering the mod requires progress elsewhere first.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="philosophers_stone" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Philosopher's Stone</Color>
</Row>

<Color id="yellow">Not a crafting table recipe any more.</Color> The Philosopher's Stone is made with <Color id="dark_purple">Malum Spirit Infusion</Color>, from an Ender Pearl with Eldritch and Arcane spirits, plus Hex Ash, Redstone, Soul Stained Steel Nuggets and Rotten Flesh.

That makes Malum the real entry requirement for ProjectE.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

| Item | Now made from |
|---|---|
| <ItemLink id="low_covalence_dust" /> | Coal plus progression stone, 40 at a time |
| <ItemLink id="alchemical_chest" /> | Covalence dusts, rose quartz, iron and progression stone |
| <ItemLink id="condenser_mk1" /> | Obsidian, rose quartz and an Alchemical Chest |
| <ItemLink id="collector_mk1" /> | Glowstone, glass, an amethyst block and a progression furnace |
| <ItemLink id="relay_mk1" /> | Obsidian, glass and an amethyst block |
| <ItemLink id="klein_star_ein" /> | Mobius Fuel around an Experience Obelisk Transforming Focus |

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Color id="gold">Two routes to MK2:</Color> the Collector MK2 and Relay MK2 each have <Color id="aqua">two</Color> recipes. One uses a Lumium Plate, the other an Ars Nouveau Jar of Light, so whichever of Jotunheim or Niflheim you tackle first will open the tier.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

The Philosopher's Stone <Color id="aqua">conversion recipes</Color> are also removed. Coal to charcoal, diamond to and from emerald, gold to and from diamond, gold to iron and iron to ender pearl no longer work in its crafting grid.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  ## <Color id="gold">World Transmutation</Color>
</Column>

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

Right-clicking blocks with the Philosopher's Stone has been retuned:

* <Color id="red">Sand and sandstone are removed from the transmutation lists entirely</Color>, so you cannot cycle sand into sandstone or back
* <Color id="green">Sand and gravel now convert into each other</Color>, in both directions
* <Color id="green">Rich Soil and Mycelium</Color> now convert into each other, in both directions

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

***

<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  ## <Color id="gold">Repair Talisman</Color>
</Column>

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="repair_talisman" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Repair Talisman</Color>
</Row>

The Repair Talisman now respects a blacklist. Items on it are skipped instead of being repaired, which stops it from trivialising gear that is meant to be consumed.

The blacklist currently holds the <Color id="aqua">Bountiful Hearts heart patches</Color>.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  ## <Color id="gold">Realm Gating</Color>
</Column>

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

ProjectE's mid and late game is spread across the realms rather than being available all at once.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

| Unlocks once you reach | What |
|---|---|
| <Color id="aqua">Bifrost</Color> | <ItemLink id="relay_mk2" /> |
| <Color id="gold">Muspelheim</Color> | <ItemLink id="transmutation_table" />, <ItemLink id="condenser_mk2" />, <ItemLink id="collector_mk3" />, <ItemLink id="relay_mk3" />, <ItemLink id="dark_matter" />, <ItemLink id="red_matter" /> |
| <Color id="red">Midgard</Color> | <ItemLink id="transmutation_tablet" />, and the whole of <Color id="light_purple">Project Expansion</Color> |

<Color id="yellow">Note that the Transmutation Table needs Muspelheim.</Color> Until you get there the Philosopher's Stone, Collectors, Relays and the Condenser are what you have, so early ProjectE is about automating condensers rather than learning items.

<Color id="red">Project Expansion is gated as a whole mod, and needs Midgard</Color>, and its matter and fuel tiers stay hidden until then. See [Project Expansion](project_expansion.md).
