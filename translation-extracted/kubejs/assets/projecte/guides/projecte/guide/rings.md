<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  ## <Color id="gold">Amulets</Color>
</Column>

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="evertide_amulet" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Evertide Amulet</Color>
</Row>

Infinite water bucket. Press the fire key to shoot a water projectile. Completely free to use.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="volcanite_amulet" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Volcanite Amulet</Color>
</Row>

Infinite lava bucket. Fire lava projectiles. Costs 32 EMC per lava placement.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  ## <Color id="gold">Rings</Color>
</Column>

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="swiftwolf_rending_gale" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Swiftwolf's Rending Gale</Color>
</Row>

Grants flight and the ability to shoot lightning. On pedestal: shoots lightning at nearby mobs.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="harvest_goddess_band" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Harvest Goddess Band</Color>
</Row>

Accelerates crop growth and auto-harvests mature crops in the surrounding area. On pedestal: same effect passively.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="black_hole_band" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Black Hole Band</Color>
</Row>

Sucks in nearby item drops. On pedestal: dumps collected items into adjacent inventories.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="ignition_ring" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Ignition Ring</Color>
</Row>

Sets nearby mobs on fire. On pedestal: same effect passively.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="zero_ring" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Zero Ring</Color>
</Row>

Freezes water and extinguishes fire and burning entities nearby.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="iron_band" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Iron Band</Color>
</Row>

No abilities on its own. Used as a crafting component for the Void Ring.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="void_ring" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Void Ring</Color>
</Row>

Combines the Black Hole Band and Gem of Eternal Density. Sucks in nearby items AND condenses them into higher EMC items automatically.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="arcana_ring" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Ring of Arcana</Color>
</Row>

Combines the Zero Ring, Ignition Ring, Harvest Goddess Band, and Swiftwolf's Rending Gale into one ring. Switch modes to select which effect is active.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>
