# <Color id="dark_purple">Project Expansion</Color>

<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  # <Color id="dark_purple">Project Expansion</Color>

  <ItemImage id="projectexpansion:final_star" scale="2" />

  Project Expansion extends ProjectE with 16 tiers of Collectors, Relays, Power Flowers, and EMC Links, plus advanced star storage, colored matter and fuel, teleportation books, and automation tools. This is the endgame of EMC progression.
</Column>

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

***

<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  ## <Color id="gold">Matter & Fuel Tiers</Color>
</Column>

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

Beyond Dark Matter and Red Matter, Project Expansion adds 12 additional matter tiers used to craft higher-tier machines. Each tier also has a corresponding fuel type.

<ItemGrid>
  <ItemIcon id="projectexpansion:magenta_matter" />
  <ItemIcon id="projectexpansion:pink_matter" />
  <ItemIcon id="projectexpansion:purple_matter" />
  <ItemIcon id="projectexpansion:violet_matter" />
  <ItemIcon id="projectexpansion:blue_matter" />
  <ItemIcon id="projectexpansion:cyan_matter" />
  <ItemIcon id="projectexpansion:green_matter" />
  <ItemIcon id="projectexpansion:lime_matter" />
  <ItemIcon id="projectexpansion:yellow_matter" />
  <ItemIcon id="projectexpansion:orange_matter" />
  <ItemIcon id="projectexpansion:white_matter" />
  <ItemIcon id="projectexpansion:fading_matter" />
</ItemGrid>

The matter progression goes: <Color id="red">Dark</Color> -> <Color id="red">Red</Color> -> <Color id="light_purple">Magenta</Color> -> <Color id="light_purple">Pink</Color> -> <Color id="dark_purple">Purple</Color> -> <Color id="dark_purple">Violet</Color> -> <Color id="aqua">Blue</Color> -> <Color id="aqua">Cyan</Color> -> <Color id="green">Green</Color> -> <Color id="green">Lime</Color> -> <Color id="gold">Yellow</Color> -> <Color id="gold">Orange</Color> -> <Color id="white">White</Color> -> Fading

Each matter tier has a block form for compact storage. The <ItemLink id="projectexpansion:matter_upgrader" /> can upgrade matter to the next tier.

<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  ## <Color id="gold">Advanced Collectors</Color>
</Column>

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

Project Expansion extends the Collector line from 3 tiers to 16. Each tier generates significantly more EMC than the last. Like base ProjectE Collectors, they generate EMC from light and upgrade fuel items.

<ItemGrid>
  <ItemIcon id="projectexpansion:basic_collector" />
  <ItemIcon id="projectexpansion:dark_collector" />
  <ItemIcon id="projectexpansion:red_collector" />
  <ItemIcon id="projectexpansion:magenta_collector" />
  <ItemIcon id="projectexpansion:pink_collector" />
  <ItemIcon id="projectexpansion:purple_collector" />
  <ItemIcon id="projectexpansion:violet_collector" />
  <ItemIcon id="projectexpansion:blue_collector" />
  <ItemIcon id="projectexpansion:cyan_collector" />
  <ItemIcon id="projectexpansion:green_collector" />
  <ItemIcon id="projectexpansion:lime_collector" />
  <ItemIcon id="projectexpansion:yellow_collector" />
  <ItemIcon id="projectexpansion:orange_collector" />
  <ItemIcon id="projectexpansion:white_collector" />
  <ItemIcon id="projectexpansion:final_collector" />
</ItemGrid>

Tiers progress through the same color sequence as matter: Basic -> Dark -> Red -> Magenta -> Pink -> Purple -> Violet -> Blue -> Cyan -> Green -> Lime -> Yellow -> Orange -> White -> Final.

<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  ## <Color id="gold">Compressed Collectors</Color>
</Column>

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<ItemGrid>
  <ItemIcon id="projectexpansion:basic_compressed_collector" />
  <ItemIcon id="projectexpansion:dark_compressed_collector" />
  <ItemIcon id="projectexpansion:red_compressed_collector" />
  <ItemIcon id="projectexpansion:final_compressed_collector" />
</ItemGrid>

Compressed Collectors cannot be placed and generate no EMC on their own. They are crafting ingredients used to build Power Flowers (covered below), which combine a Collector and a Relay into a single block. Available in all 16 tiers.


<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  ## <Color id="gold">Advanced Relays</Color>
</Column>

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<ItemGrid>
  <ItemIcon id="projectexpansion:basic_relay" />
  <ItemIcon id="projectexpansion:dark_relay" />
  <ItemIcon id="projectexpansion:red_relay" />

  ## <Color id="gold">Power Flowers</Color>
</Column>

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="projectexpansion:basic_power_flower" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Power Flower</Color>
</Row>

Power Flowers combine Collectors and Relays into a single block. They passively generate EMC on a timer without needing light. Available in all 16 tiers, they are the most convenient way to generate EMC at scale.

<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  ## <Color id="gold">EMC Links</Color>
</Column>

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="projectexpansion:basic_emc_link" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">EMC Link</Color>
</Row>

EMC Links bridge your personal EMC network with automation systems. They can import items (converting them to EMC), export items (spending EMC to create them), and handle fluids. Available in 16 tiers with increasing transfer limits per operation.

<ItemGrid>
  <ItemIcon id="projectexpansion:basic_emc_link" />
  <ItemIcon id="projectexpansion:dark_emc_link" />
  <ItemIcon id="projectexpansion:red_emc_link" />
  <ItemIcon id="projectexpansion:final_emc_link" />
</ItemGrid>

Pipe items into an EMC Link to convert them to EMC automatically. Set a target item and pipe out to produce items on demand. Higher tiers handle more items and fluids per tick.


<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  ## <Color id="gold">Alchemical Books</Color>
</Column>

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

Alchemical Books are teleportation items that let you save and warp to locations, even across dimensions. Four tiers offer progressively cheaper teleportation costs.

<ItemGrid>
  <ItemIcon id="projectexpansion:basic_alchemical_book" />
  <ItemIcon id="projectexpansion:advanced_alchemical_book" />
  <ItemIcon id="projectexpansion:master_alchemical_book" />
  <ItemIcon id="projectexpansion:arcane_alchemical_book" />
</ItemGrid>

- <ItemLink id="projectexpansion:basic_alchemical_book" /> — Standard EMC cost per block
- <ItemLink id="projectexpansion:advanced_alchemical_book" /> — <Color id="green">50%</Color> cheaper teleportation
- <ItemLink id="projectexpansion:master_alchemical_book" /> — <Color id="green">80%</Color> cheaper teleportation
- <ItemLink id="projectexpansion:arcane_alchemical_book" /> — <Color id="green">Free</Color> teleportation

Books can be bound to other players for quick warping to teammates. Store multiple waypoints in a single book.


<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

  ## <Color id="gold">Utility Items</Color>
</Column>

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="projectexpansion:transmutation_interface" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Transmutation Interface</Color>
</Row>

Connects your personal transmutation network to automation systems like Applied Energistics 2. Pipe systems can request and insert items directly through your EMC knowledge.

<Color id="red">Requires Midgard stage.</Color>


<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="projectexpansion:matter_upgrader" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Matter Upgrader</Color>
</Row>

Converts matter items to the next tier up. Insert lower-tier matter and it will be upgraded automatically.


<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="projectexpansion:compact_sun" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Compact Sun</Color>
</Row>

Multiplies the output of certain blocks placed below or above it. Use with Collectors or Power Flowers for boosted EMC generation.


<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="projectexpansion:knowledge_sharing_book" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Knowledge Sharing Book</Color>
</Row>

Share your learned transmutation recipes with other players. Give one to a friend to copy your item knowledge to them.


<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="projectexpansion:infinite_fuel" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Infinite Fuel</Color>
</Row>

A fuel source that never runs out but costs EMC to use. Place it in a furnace or any fuel-consuming machine for unlimited smelting power.


<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="projectexpansion:infinite_steak" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Infinite Steak</Color>
