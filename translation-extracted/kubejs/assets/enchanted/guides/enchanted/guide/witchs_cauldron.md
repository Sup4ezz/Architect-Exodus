  # <Color id="dark_purple">Witch's Cauldron</Color>
  <ItemImage id="cauldron" scale="2" />

  The Witch's Cauldron is a brewing vessel distinct from the Kettle. It is used for the more potent magical reagents — most importantly, <ItemLink id="mutandis" />.
</Column>

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

***

<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  ## <Color id="gold">Using the Cauldron</Color>
</Column>

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="cauldron" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Witch's Cauldron</Color>
</Row>

Place the Witch's Cauldron over a heat source (Campfire, lit Netherrack, etc.) and fill it with water — either with a bucket, or by leaving it in the rain. Once the water is heated, drop the ingredients for the recipe into the cauldron. Successful brews produce the output item floating above the cauldron.

Check JEI for the full list of cauldron recipes — the cauldron is used for a number of magical brews beyond Mutandis.

<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  ## <Color id="gold">Mutandis</Color>
</Column>

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="mutandis" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Mutandis</Color>
</Row>

Mutandis is one of the most important items in Enchanted. It is brewed in the Witch's Cauldron and used to transform plants and saplings into their magical counterparts.

Right-click Mutandis on saplings or plants to randomly transform them into magical variants — this is how you obtain the magical trees and special plants needed for progression.

<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  ## <Color id="gold">Mutandis Plants</Color>
</Column>

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

Use Mutandis on regular saplings to transform them into magical tree saplings, or on small plants (flowers, grass, mushrooms) to create magical plant variants.

- <ItemLink id="rowan_sapling" /> — The most commonly needed magical tree. Rowan wood is used to craft Altar blocks, Distilleries, and many other items.
- <ItemLink id="alder_sapling" /> — Alder wood is used in various crafting recipes.
- <ItemLink id="hawthorn_sapling" /> — Hawthorn wood is used in select recipes and is needed for certain advanced items.
- <ItemLink id="spanish_moss" /> — A hanging plant that can be found or created with Mutandis.
- <ItemLink id="ember_moss" /> — A fiery plant variant obtained through Mutandis.
- <ItemLink id="glint_weed" /> — A glowing plant that emits light. Useful for decoration and some recipes.
- <ItemLink id="blood_poppy" /> — A dark flower used in certain advanced recipes.
