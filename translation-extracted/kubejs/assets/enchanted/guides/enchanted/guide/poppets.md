# <Color id="dark_purple">Poppets</Color>

<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  # <Color id="dark_purple">Poppets</Color>
  <ItemImage id="poppet" scale="2" />

  Poppets are magical dolls that can be bound to a player and provide various protective effects. They are a core defensive system in Enchanted.
</Column>

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

***

<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  ## <Color id="gold">Crafting Poppets</Color>
</Column>

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

Creating Poppets requires several components:

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="spinning_wheel" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Spinning Wheel</Color>
</Row>

Used to spin magical thread needed for Poppet crafting. This is a required crafting station for the Poppet system.


<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="poppet" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Poppet</Color>
</Row>

The base Poppet doll. This is the foundation that gets infused into specific Poppet types with different protective effects.


<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

***

<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  ## <Color id="gold">Using Poppets</Color>
</Column>

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="taglock_kit" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Taglock Kit</Color>
</Row>

The Taglock Kit is used to bind a Poppet to a specific player. Right-click on a player (or yourself) with the Taglock Kit to collect a sample, then combine it with a Poppet to bind the effect to that player.


<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="poppet_shelf" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Poppet Shelf</Color>
</Row>

The Poppet Shelf is a storage block for your active Poppets. Place bound Poppets on the shelf to activate their protective effects. A Poppet must be on a shelf to function.


<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

***

<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  ## <Color id="gold">Poppet Types</Color>
</Column>

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="infused_armour_poppet" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Infused Armour Poppet</Color>
</Row>

One of several Poppet types available. Each Poppet type provides a different protective effect, such as damage reduction, death protection, or fire resistance. Infuse base Poppets at the Altar using different ingredients to create specific types.
