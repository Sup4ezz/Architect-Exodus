<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  ## <Color id="gold">Kettle Recipes</Color>
</Column>

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="kettle" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Kettle</Color>
</Row>

Place the Kettle over a fire source (such as a Campfire or lit Netherrack) and fill it with water using a bucket or by placing it in the rain. Once heated, toss ingredients into the bubbling water to brew recipes.


<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="redstone_soup" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Redstone Soup</Color>
</Row>

Redstone Soup is a consumable brewed in the Kettle. It provides useful effects when consumed. Check JEI for the full list of items that can be tossed into the Kettle.

<Color id="yellow">Note:</Color> <ItemLink id="enchanted:mutandis" /> is <Color id="red">not</Color> brewed in the Kettle — it requires the [Witch's Cauldron](witchs_cauldron.md).
