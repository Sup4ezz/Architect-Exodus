  # <Color id="dark_purple">Enchanted</Color>

  <ItemImage id="witch_oven" scale="2" />

  Enchanted is a witchcraft mod that lets you brew potions in a Kettle, smelt magical byproducts in a Witch's Oven, craft Poppets for protection, perform Rites using chalk circles, and even fly on an enchanted broomstick.
</Column>

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

***

<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  ## <Color id="gold">Progression Overview</Color>
</Column>

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.5"/>

1. Gather witchcraft herbs: <ItemLink id="mandrake_seeds" />, <ItemLink id="belladonna_seeds" />, <ItemLink id="snowbell_seeds" />, and more
2. Build a <ItemLink id="kettle" /> for general brews like <ItemLink id="redstone_soup" />
3. Build a <ItemLink id="cauldron" /> and brew <ItemLink id="mutandis" /> in it
4. Use Mutandis to grow magical trees: <ItemLink id="rowan_sapling" />, <ItemLink id="alder_sapling" />, <ItemLink id="hawthorn_sapling" />
5. Build a <ItemLink id="witch_oven" /> and collect magical byproducts
6. Craft an <ItemLink id="attuned_stone" /> and build the <ItemLink id="altar" />
7. Set up a <ItemLink id="distillery" /> for advanced ingredients
8. Craft <ItemLink id="ritual_chalk" /> and perform Rites
9. Create <ItemLink id="poppet" /> for magical protection
10. Brew <ItemLink id="flying_ointment" /> and take flight on an <ItemLink id="enchanted_broomstick" />
