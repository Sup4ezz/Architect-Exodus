# <Color id="dark_purple">Witch's Oven</Color>

<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  # <Color id="dark_purple">Witch's Oven</Color>
  <ItemImage id="witch_oven" scale="2" />

  The Witch's Oven is a special furnace that smelts items like a normal furnace but also produces magical byproducts as a side effect. These byproducts are essential ingredients for advanced witchcraft.
</Column>

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

***

<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  ## <Color id="gold">The Witch's Oven</Color>
</Column>

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="witch_oven" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Witch's Oven</Color>
</Row>

The Witch's Oven functions like a furnace — feed it items and fuel. The key difference is that it has an extra output slot where magical byproducts collect in <ItemLink id="clay_jar" />.

You must provide empty Clay Jars for the byproducts to be captured. Without jars, the byproducts are lost.


<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="clay_jar" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Clay Jars</Color>
</Row>

Clay Jars are placed in the Witch's Oven to capture magical byproducts during smelting. Keep a good supply on hand — you will need many of them.


<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

***

<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  ## <Color id="gold">Fume Funnel & Filter</Color>
</Column>

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="fume_funnel" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Fume Funnel</Color>
</Row>

The Fume Funnel is an upgrade placed adjacent to the Witch's Oven. It increases the speed of the oven and improves the chance of producing byproducts.


<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="fume_filter" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Fume Filter</Color>
</Row>

The Fume Filter is placed inside a Fume Funnel to further increase the chance of obtaining specific byproducts. Use it to target the byproduct you need.


<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

***

<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  ## <Color id="gold">Magical Byproducts</Color>
</Column>

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

Different items smelted in the Witch's Oven produce different byproducts:

<ItemGrid>
  <ItemIcon id="exhale_of_the_horned_one" />
  <ItemIcon id="breath_of_the_goddess" />
  <ItemIcon id="whiff_of_magic" />
  <ItemIcon id="odour_of_purity" />
  <ItemIcon id="hint_of_rebirth" />
  <ItemIcon id="reek_of_misfortune" />
  <ItemIcon id="foul_fume" />
</ItemGrid>

- <ItemLink id="exhale_of_the_horned_one" /> — A common byproduct needed for many recipes. Obtained by smelting logs and other wood items.
- <ItemLink id="breath_of_the_goddess" /> — Another essential byproduct. Obtained by smelting various items in the oven.
- <ItemLink id="whiff_of_magic" /> — Used in advanced crafting recipes.
- <ItemLink id="odour_of_purity" /> — Used in purification recipes.
- <ItemLink id="hint_of_rebirth" /> — Used in recipes related to renewal and transformation.
- <ItemLink id="reek_of_misfortune" /> — Used in curse-related recipes.
- <ItemLink id="foul_fume" /> — A noxious byproduct used in dark witchcraft recipes.
