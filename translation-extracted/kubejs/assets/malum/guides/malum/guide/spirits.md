# <Color id="dark_purple">Spirit Arcana</Color>

<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  # <Color id="dark_purple">Spirit Arcana</Color>
  <ItemImage id="sacred_spirit" scale="2" />

  Spirit Arcana are the essence of souls, harvested from slain creatures using a Scythe. Different creatures drop different types and combinations of spirits. Spirits are the fuel and ingredient for all of Malum's magic systems.
</Column>

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

***

<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  ## <Color id="gold">Spirit Types</Color>
</Column>

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="sacred_spirit" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Sacred Spirit</Color>
</Row>

Obtained from peaceful, holy creatures such as villagers and iron golems. Associated with healing and protection.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="wicked_spirit" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Wicked Spirit</Color>
</Row>

Obtained from hostile, undead creatures such as zombies, skeletons, and wither skeletons. Associated with harm and darkness.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="arcane_spirit" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Arcane Spirit</Color>
</Row>

The most common type, obtained from many creatures. The universal spirit used in basic recipes.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="earthen_spirit" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Earthen Spirit</Color>
</Row>

Obtained from earth-bound creatures. Associated with strength and durability.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="infernal_spirit" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Infernal Spirit</Color>
</Row>

Obtained from Nether creatures such as blazes and magma cubes. Associated with fire and heat.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="aerial_spirit" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Aerial Spirit</Color>
</Row>

Obtained from flying or fast creatures such as phantoms and bats. Associated with speed and movement.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="aqueous_spirit" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Aqueous Spirit</Color>
</Row>

Obtained from water creatures such as guardians and drowned. Associated with water and fluidity.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="eldritch_spirit" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Eldritch Spirit</Color>
</Row>

Obtained from End creatures such as endermen and shulkers. Rare and powerful, used in advanced recipes.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

***

<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  ## <Color id="gold">Collecting Spirits</Color>
</Column>

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

Kill mobs with any Scythe to collect their spirits. Different mobs drop different spirit combinations. A <ItemLink id="spirit_pouch" /> automatically collects dropped spirits. The Soulhunter armor set increases spirit drops.

><Color id="yellow">Important:</Color> Without a Scythe equipped, slain creatures will not drop any spirits. Always carry one when hunting for Spirit Arcana.
