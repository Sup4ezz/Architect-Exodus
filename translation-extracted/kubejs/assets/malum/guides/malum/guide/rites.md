# <Color id="dark_purple">Totem Rites</Color>

<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  # <Color id="dark_purple">Totem Rites</Color>
  <ItemImage id="runewood_totem_base" scale="2" />

  Totem Rites are area-of-effect magic performed using Totem poles. Stack spirit Runes on a Totem Base to activate powerful continuous effects in the surrounding area.
</Column>

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

***

<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  ## <Color id="gold">How Rites Work</Color>
</Column>

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="runewood_totem_base" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Runewood Totem Base</Color>
</Row>

Place a <ItemLink id="runewood_totem_base" />, then stack Runewood Totem Poles with specific spirit Runes on top. The combination of runes determines which rite is performed. When activated, the rite provides a continuous effect to the area around the totem.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="spirit_jar" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Spirit Jar</Color>
</Row>

A <ItemLink id="spirit_jar" /> placed near a totem stores excess spirits and can fuel rites that consume spirits over time. Useful for keeping rites active without manual resupply.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="soulwood_totem_base" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Soulwood Totem Base</Color>
</Row>

The <Color id="dark_purple">Unchained Rite</Color> (using all 5 rune types) converts the Runewood Totem into Soulwood, altering the terrain into Blight. A <ItemLink id="soulwood_totem_base" /> produces corrupted versions of every rite with different effects.

  ## <Color id="gold">Rites</Color>
</Column>

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

### <Color id="aqua">Elemental Rites</Color>

The basic elemental rites available with a Runewood Totem:

- <Color id="yellow">Sacred Rite</Color> — Heals nearby friendly entities.
- <Color id="dark_gray">Wicked Rite</Color> — Damages nearby hostile entities.
- <Color id="dark_green">Earthen Rite</Color> — Grants nearby friendlies increased armor.
- <Color id="red">Infernal Rite</Color> — Grants fire resistance and extinguishes flames.
- <Color id="white">Aerial Rite</Color> — Increases movement speed of nearby friendlies.
- <Color id="blue">Aqueous Rite</Color> — Extends the reach of nearby friendlies.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.55"/>

### <Color id="aqua">Soulwood & Corrupted Rites</Color>

Corrupted rites available with a Soulwood Totem:

- <Color id="yellow">Corrupted Sacred</Color> — Accelerates animal growth and feeds animals for breeding.
- <Color id="dark_gray">Corrupted Wicked</Color> — Buffs nearby hostiles with strength, speed, and resistance.
- <Color id="dark_green">Corrupted Earthen</Color> — Increases damage dealt by nearby friendlies.
- <Color id="red">Corrupted Infernal</Color> — Extinguishes fire and heals burned entities.
- <Color id="white">Corrupted Aerial</Color> — Decreases gravity and increases jump height.
- <Color id="blue">Corrupted Aqueous</Color> — Improves fishing with Lure I and Luck of the Sea I.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.5"/>

### <Color id="aqua">Greater Rites</Color>

Greater rites are advanced versions of the corrupted rites, providing more powerful or specialized effects:

- <Color id="yellow">Greater Corrupted Sacred</Color> — Auto-breeds animals in range, capping at 20 per type.
- <Color id="dark_gray">Greater Corrupted Wicked</Color> — Culls overcrowded animals above 20 per type.
- <Color id="dark_green">Greater Corrupted Earthen</Color> — Creates cobblestone from nothing.
- <Color id="red">Greater Corrupted Infernal</Color> — Speeds up nearby furnaces.
- <Color id="white">Greater Corrupted Aerial</Color> — Reduces insomnia and phantom spawning.
- <Color id="blue">Greater Corrupted Aqueous</Color> — Converts zombies to drowned.
