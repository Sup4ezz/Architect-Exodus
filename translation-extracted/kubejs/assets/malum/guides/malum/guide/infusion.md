<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  # <Color id="dark_purple">Infusion</Color>
  <ItemImage id="spirit_crucible" scale="2" />

  Advanced crafting in Malum revolves around Spirit Infusion at the altar and transmutation at the Spirit Crucible. This page covers both stations, their upgrades, and key materials.
</Column>

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

***

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  ## <Color id="gold">Spirit Infusion</Color>
</Column>

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="spirit_altar" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Spirit Altar</Color>
</Row>

Spirit Infusion is performed at the <ItemLink id="spirit_altar" />. Place the target item on the altar, surrounding ingredients on Arcane Pedestals, and spirits on additional pedestals. The altar consumes everything to create the result. Key products include Hex Ash, Hallowed Gold, Soulstained Steel, and many tools and trinkets.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.5"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="runewood_obelisk" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Runewood Obelisk</Color>
</Row>

Each one increases the Spirit Altar processing speed by 25%. Up to 4 can be placed near the Spirit Altar.

  ## <Color id="gold">Infused Metals</Color>
</Column>

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="soul_stained_steel_ingot" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Soulstained Steel</Color>
</Row>

Soulstained Steel is created from iron with Wicked spirits. It is used for offensive and dark items, including powerful weapons. Both metals are essential for advancing through Malum.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="hallowed_gold_ingot" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Hallowed Gold</Color>
</Row>

<Color id="yellow">Hallowed Gold</Color> is created through Spirit Infusion of gold with Sacred spirits. It is used for protective and holy items, including the Runewood Obelisk. An essential material for defensive progression.

<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  ## <Color id="gold">Spirit Crucible</Color>
</Column>

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="spirit_crucible" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Spirit Crucible</Color>
</Row>

The <ItemLink id="spirit_crucible" /> is an advanced crafting station used for transmutation and focusing. Place an item inside, provide spirits, and the crucible focuses them into the target. Used for creating advanced materials. Slower than the altar but handles more complex recipes.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.5"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="spirit_catalyzer" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Spirit Catalyzer</Color>
</Row>

Speeds up the crucible. Up to 8 can be placed nearby, but more catalyzers cause instability, dealing extra damage to the impetus or catalyst. Requires fuel in the form of spirits consumed during operation.

Additional crucible enhancements:

- **Blazing Diode** — Lessens fuel requirements of catalyzers while slightly speeding the process.
- **Accelerating Inlay** — Provides a substantial bonus to focusing speed without drawbacks.
- **Crucible Augmentation** — <ItemLink id="alchemical_calx" />-based foci that add unique effects. Up to 4 can be placed in the crucible. Use a Tuning Fork to view and control augment resonances.
