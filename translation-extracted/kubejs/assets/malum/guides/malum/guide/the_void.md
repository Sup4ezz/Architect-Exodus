<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  ## <Color id="gold">Soulwood</Color>
</Column>

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="soulwood_log" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Soulwood</Color>
</Row>

Created when a Runewood Totem undergoes the Unchained Rite (Arcane Rite). The violent process scars the wood, warping its magical nature. <ItemLink id="soulwood_log" /> totems produce corrupted rite effects, turning familiar rituals into something darker and more powerful. Soulwood can be propagated by placing Runewood on blighted terrain.

<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  ## <Color id="gold">Blight</Color>
</Column>

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="blighted_gunk" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Blight</Color>
</Row>

The substance created by the Unchained Rite. Blight isn't harmful, not in of itself — it's just gunk. But it has a spiritual memory and can spread when given arcana or fertilizer. Blight is important for the Unchained Rite — it resonates with the violence of its creation, and blocks placed on blight during a Corrupted Unchained Rite can be transmuted into something new.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

  ## <Color id="gold">Eldritch Arcana</Color>
</Column>

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="null_slate" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Eldritch Arcana</Color>
</Row>

The most advanced magic in Malum. For every push there is a pull. Eldritch Arcana combines void and soul magic for powerful but unpredictable results. Requires mastery of both the Soul path and the Void path. The <ItemLink id="null_slate" /> serves as a foundation for these advanced workings.

  ## <Color id="gold">Mirror Magic</Color>
</Column>

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="mnemonic_fragment" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Mirror Magic</Color>
</Row>

An advanced technique that uses the reflection of arcana for unique effects and crafting processes. Mirror magic manipulates the duality inherent in all spirit arcana — every spirit has an echo, and those who master the Void can harness that echo. <ItemLink id="mnemonic_fragment" /> is a key component in these reflective workings.
