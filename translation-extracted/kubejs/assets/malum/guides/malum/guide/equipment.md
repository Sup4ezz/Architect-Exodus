  # <Color id="dark_purple">Equipment & Trinkets</Color>
  <ItemImage id="tyrving" scale="2" />

  Malum offers a range of soul-forged weapons, armor sets, and trinkets. Each piece is designed to complement the spirit harvesting playstyle, from scythes that reap souls to trinkets that enhance your magical capabilities.
</Column>

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

***

<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  ## <Color id="gold">Scythes</Color>
</Column>

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

The core weapons of Malum. Scythes are required to harvest spirits from slain mobs — without one, spirits are lost.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="crude_scythe" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Crude Scythe</Color>
</Row>

Your starter scythe with basic stats. Enough to begin collecting spirits, but you will want to upgrade as soon as possible.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="soul_stained_steel_scythe" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Soul Stained Steel Scythe</Color>
</Row>

The upgraded version with better damage and improved spirit harvesting. Crafted through Spirit Infusion using Soulstained Steel.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="tyrving" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Tyrving</Color>
</Row>

An ancient blade infused with powerful dark magic properties. A devastating weapon for those willing to embrace the deeper arts.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

  ## <Color id="gold">Armor Sets</Color>
</Column>

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="soul_stained_steel_chestplate" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Soulstained Steel Armor</Color>
</Row>

Heavy combat armor crafted from soul-infused steel. Provides <Color id="dark_purple">Soul Ward</Color> — a magical shield that absorbs damage using arcane energy. Soul Ward regenerates over time after not being hit. A full set consists of <ItemLink id="soul_stained_steel_helmet" />, <ItemLink id="soul_stained_steel_chestplate" />, <ItemLink id="soul_stained_steel_leggings" />, and <ItemLink id="soul_stained_steel_boots" />.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="soul_hunter_cloak" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Soul Hunter Set</Color>
</Row>

Light armor focused on spirit collection. Increases the quantity and variety of spirits dropped by slain mobs, making it essential for farming spirits efficiently. The set includes <ItemLink id="soul_hunter_cloak" />, <ItemLink id="soul_hunter_robe" />, <ItemLink id="soul_hunter_leggings" />, and <ItemLink id="soul_hunter_boots" />.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

***

<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  ## <Color id="gold">Soul Ward</Color>
</Column>

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

A unique mechanic provided by Soulstained Steel armor. Soul Ward acts as an extra layer of health that absorbs incoming damage before your regular health is touched. It regenerates after a delay when you stop taking damage. Various trinkets and the <ItemLink id="belt_of_the_magebane" /> can improve its recovery speed and capacity.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

## <Color id="aqua">Belts</Color>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="belt_of_the_starved" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Belt of the Starved</Color>
</Row>

Drains hunger faster but greatly increases magic proficiency. Synergizes with rotten foods and Concentrated Gluttony items.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="belt_of_the_prospector" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Belt of the Prospector</Color>
</Row>

Explosions you cause apply Fortune III and don't destroy items. Pairs with Ring of the Demolitionist (bigger explosions) and Ring of the Hoarder (loot teleports to you).

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="belt_of_the_magebane" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Belt of the Magebane</Color>
</Row>

Improves Soul Ward recovery rate and capacity. Converts incoming magic damage into Soul Ward recovery.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>
