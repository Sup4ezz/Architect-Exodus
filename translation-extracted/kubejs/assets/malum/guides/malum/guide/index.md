  # <Color id="dark_purple">Malum</Color>

  <ItemImage id="encyclopedia_arcana" scale="2" />

  Malum is a dark magic mod centered around the harvesting and manipulation of spirits. Slay creatures with a Scythe to collect <Color id="dark_purple">Spirit Arcana</Color> — the essence of souls. Use Spirit Infusion at the Spirit Altar to craft powerful items, perform Totem Rites for area effects, and transmute materials at the Spirit Crucible. Features Soulstained Steel and Hallowed Gold equipment, soul-powered trinkets, and the forbidden arts of Soulwood.
