  # <Color id="red">Материалы</Color>
  <ItemImage id="thermal:tin_ingot" scale="2" />
  Thermal Foundation добавляет руды, металлы, сплавы и драгоценные камни, используемые во всей серии Thermal. Многие материалы унифицированы и применяются в разных модах.
</Column>

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

***

<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  ## <Color id="gold">Основные металлы</Color>
</Column>

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

Эти руды генерируются в мире и добываются обычным способом. Перерабатывайте их в <ItemLink id="thermal:machine_pulverizer" />, чтобы получать двойной выход.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="thermal:tin_ingot" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Tin</Color>
</Row>

Распространённый металл для создания бронзового сплава и базовых компонентов механизмов. Встречается на большинстве подземных уровней.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="thermal:lead_ingot" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Lead</Color>
</Row>

Плотный металл, используемый в укреплённых корпусах механизмов and energy storage. Pairs with Silver to create various components.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="thermal:silver_ingot" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Silver</Color>
</Row>

A conductive metal used in Electrum alloy crafting and advanced circuitry.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="thermal:nickel_ingot" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Nickel</Color>
</Row>

Used to create Constantan and Invar alloys. Less common than Tin or Lead.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  ## <Color id="gold">Alloys</Color>
</Column>

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

Alloys are created in the <ItemLink id="thermal:machine_smelter" /> by combining base metals. They unlock progressively more powerful machines and augments.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="thermal:bronze_ingot" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Bronze</Color>
</Row>

Copper + Tin. An early alloy used in basic components and some machine recipes.


<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="thermal:electrum_ingot" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Electrum</Color>
</Row>

Gold + Silver. Used in Redstone Flux components and mid-tier machine parts.


<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="thermal:invar_ingot" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Invar</Color>
</Row>

Iron + Nickel. A sturdy alloy used in Reinforced machine casings and durable components.


<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="thermal:constantan_ingot" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Constantan</Color>
</Row>

Copper + Nickel. Used in thermal regulation components and some machine augments.


<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="thermal:signalum_ingot" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Signalum</Color>
</Row>

Copper + Silver + Redstone. An advanced alloy used in high-tier augments and Resonant components. Requires the Induction Smelter.


<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="thermal:lumium_ingot" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Lumium</Color>
</Row>

Tin + Silver + Glowstone. A luminous alloy used in advanced augments and lighting components.


<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="thermal:enderium_ingot" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Enderium</Color>
</Row>

Lead + Diamond + Ender Pearl. The most advanced Thermal alloy. Required for Resonant-tier upgrades and endgame augments.


<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  ## <Color id="gold">Processing Forms</Color>
</Column>

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

Thermal Foundation metals can be processed into multiple forms using the Press:

- <Color id="aqua">Plates</Color> — Flat metal sheets used in machine casings and augments
- <Color id="aqua">Gears</Color> — Toothed components used in machine internals (requires the Gear Die in the Press)
- <Color id="aqua">Dusts</Color> — Powdered metals from the Pulverizer, can be smelted back into ingots
- <Color id="aqua">Nuggets</Color> — Small fragments, 9 combine into an ingot
