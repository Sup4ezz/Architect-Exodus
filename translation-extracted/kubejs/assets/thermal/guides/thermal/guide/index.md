<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  # <Color id="red">Thermal Series</Color>

  <ItemImage id="thermal:machine_frame" scale="2" />

  The Thermal Series is a collection of tech mods covering resource processing, power generation, fluid handling, automation, and farming. Smelt alloys, crush ores, generate power with Dynamos, and upgrade everything with Augments.
</Column>

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Color id="red">Unlocked at Jotunheim stage.</Color>

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

***

<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  ## <Color id="gold">General Progression</Color>
</Column>

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.5"/>

1. Craft a <ItemLink id="thermal:machine_frame" /> — the foundation of every Thermal machine
2. Build a <ItemLink id="thermal:dynamo_stirling" /> for early power using furnace fuels
3. Set up a <ItemLink id="thermal:machine_pulverizer" /> for ore doubling and a <ItemLink id="thermal:machine_furnace" /> for FE-powered smelting
4. Use the <ItemLink id="thermal:machine_smelter" /> to create alloys like <ItemLink id="thermal:invar_ingot" />, <ItemLink id="thermal:electrum_ingot" />, and <ItemLink id="thermal:signalum_ingot" />
5. Upgrade machines with <ItemLink id="thermal:upgrade_augment_1" /> through <ItemLink id="thermal:upgrade_augment_3" /> for increased speed
6. Install specialized Augments for speed, efficiency, and bonus output
7. Scale up power with advanced Dynamos and store it in <ItemLink id="thermal:energy_cell" />
8. Expand into farming with the <ItemLink id="thermal:machine_insolator" /> and Thermal Cultivation crops

  ## <Color id="gold">Included Mods</Color>
</Column>

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

- <Color id="aqua">Thermal Foundation</Color> — Ores, metals, alloys, gems, and crafting materials
- <Color id="aqua">Thermal Expansion</Color> — Machines, Dynamos, storage, and Augments
- <Color id="aqua">Thermal Cultivation</Color> — Crops, seeds, and the Phytogenic Insolator
- <Color id="aqua">Thermal Dynamics</Color> — Ducts for item, fluid, and energy transport
- <Color id="aqua">Thermal Innovation</Color> — Portable tools powered by Redstone Flux
- <Color id="aqua">Thermal Extra</Color> — Extended augment tiers and additional machines
