  Thermal Cultivation adds a variety of crops, seeds, and food items. Grow them in the ground or automate production with the <ItemLink id="thermal:machine_insolator" />.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

***

<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  ## <Color id="gold">Growing Crops</Color>
</Column>

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

All Thermal Cultivation crops grow on standard Farmland and follow vanilla farming rules — till soil, plant seeds, provide water and light, then wait for growth. Use Bone Meal or the <ItemLink id="thermal:machine_insolator" /> to speed things up.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

The <Color id="aqua">Phytogenic Insolator</Color> is the best way to farm these crops at scale. Feed it seeds, Water, and optionally <ItemLink id="thermal:apatite" /> as fertilizer for dramatically faster growth and bonus seed/crop drops.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>
