<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  ## <Color id="gold">Thermal Dynamics — Ducts</Color>
</Column>

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

Thermal Dynamics adds duct-based transport — an alternative to EnderIO Conduits or standard piping. Ducts connect machines, storage, and generators for item, fluid, and energy transfer.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

- <Color id="aqua">Flux Ducts</Color> — Transport Redstone Flux (energy) between Dynamos, machines, and RF Cells. Available in multiple tiers with increasing transfer rates: Leadstone, Hardened, Redstone, Signalum, Resonant, and Cryo-Stabilized (infinite transfer).

- <Color id="aqua">Fluiducts</Color> — Move fluids between tanks, machines, and fluid storage. Temperate Fluiducts handle cold fluids; Hardened Fluiducts handle hot fluids like lava. Super-Laminar Fluiducts provide unlimited transfer rate.

- <Color id="aqua">Itemducts</Color> — Transport items between inventories. Standard Itemducts move items at a set speed. Warp Itemducts teleport items instantly. Use Servos to extract items and Filters to control routing.

- <Color id="aqua">Servos & Filters</Color> — Attach to duct connections to control extraction (Servos) and destination filtering (Filters). Available in multiple tiers: Basic, Hardened, Reinforced, Signalum, and Resonant — each tier adds more filter slots and configuration options.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Color id="gold">Tip:</Color> Unlike EnderIO Conduits, different duct types cannot share a single block space. Plan your duct routing accordingly or use Covers (cosmetic facades) to clean up the appearance.

<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  ## <Color id="gold">Thermal Innovation — Portable Tools</Color>
</Column>

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

Thermal Innovation adds RF-powered portable tools that run on stored Redstone Flux instead of durability. Charge them in an Energetic Infuser or by placing them in a powered machine's augment slot.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

- <Color id="aqua">Fluxbore</Color> — An RF-powered pickaxe/shovel hybrid. Mines blocks using stored energy. Upgrade with augments for larger mining area (up to 3x3) and higher efficiency.

- <Color id="aqua">Fluxsaw</Color> — An RF-powered axe. Chops trees and breaks wood blocks using stored energy. Can be upgraded for wider area of effect.

- <Color id="aqua">Fluxomagnet</Color> — A magnetic item collector powered by RF. Pulls dropped items toward you from a configurable range. Toggle on/off with right-click.

- <Color id="aqua">Hypoinfuser</Color> — An RF-powered potion applicator. Load it with potions and it automatically applies them to you when the effect expires. Convenient for maintaining permanent buffs.
