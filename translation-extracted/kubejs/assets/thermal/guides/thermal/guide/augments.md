# <Color id="red">Augments</Color>

<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  # <Color id="red">Augments</Color>
  <ItemImage id="thermal:upgrade_augment_1" scale="2" />
  Augments are the upgrade system for every Thermal machine and Dynamo. Install them in the augment slots of any device to boost speed, efficiency, output, and energy handling.
</Column>

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

***

<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  ## <Color id="gold">Integral Components (Tier Upgrades)</Color>
</Column>

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

Integral Components are the primary scaling mechanism. Each tier multiplies your machine's base speed and energy capacity. Install one to unlock a machine's full potential.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="thermal:upgrade_augment_1" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Integral Components</Color>
</Row>

Tier 1 upgrade. Doubles machine processing speed and energy storage. Your first augment priority.


<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="thermal:upgrade_augment_2" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Reinforced Integral Components</Color>
</Row>

Tier 2 upgrade. Triples machine speed. Requires more advanced materials to craft.


<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="thermal:upgrade_augment_3" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Resonant Integral Components</Color>
</Row>

Tier 3 upgrade. Quadruples machine speed. Requires <ItemLink id="thermal:enderium_ingot" /> and <ItemLink id="thermal:lumium_ingot" /> — the highest base-game tier.


<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

***

<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  ## <Color id="gold">Thermal Extra Tiers</Color>
</Column>

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

The Thermal Extra addon extends the upgrade chain beyond Resonant with two additional tiers:

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="thermal_extra:upgrade_augment" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Thermal Extra Integral Components</Color>
</Row>

Tier 4 upgrade. Further increases machine performance beyond Resonant.


<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="thermal_extra:abyssal_upgrade_augment" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Abyssal Integral Components</Color>
</Row>

Tier 5 — the ultimate upgrade. Maximum machine performance. Requires endgame materials.


<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

***

<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  ## <Color id="gold">Machine Augments</Color>
</Column>

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

Specialized augments that modify how machines operate. Each type is available in base Thermal and in higher tiers from Thermal Extra (Soul Infused, Twinite, Shellite, Dragonsteel, Abyssal).

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

| Augment | Effect |
|---|---|
| <ItemImage id="thermal:machine_speed_augment" scale="0.5" /> <Color id="aqua">Linkage Amplifier</Color> | Increases processing speed at the cost of higher energy consumption |
| <ItemImage id="thermal:machine_efficiency_augment" scale="0.5" /> <Color id="aqua">Flux Efficiency</Color> | Reduces energy consumption per operation |
| <ItemImage id="thermal:machine_output_augment" scale="0.5" /> <Color id="aqua">Auxiliary Process Sieve</Color> | Increases bonus/secondary output chance |
| <ItemImage id="thermal:machine_catalyst_augment" scale="0.5" /> <Color id="aqua">Reclamation Chamber</Color> | Reduces catalyst consumption in machines that use catalysts |

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

***

<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  ## <Color id="gold">RF Coil Augments</Color>
</Column>

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

RF Coils improve the energy handling of machines, Dynamos, and RF Cells. Available in base and Thermal Extra tiers.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

| Augment | Effect |
|---|---|
| <ItemImage id="thermal:rf_coil_augment" scale="0.5" /> <Color id="aqua">Expanded RF Coil</Color> | Increases energy capacity and transfer rate |
| <ItemImage id="thermal:rf_coil_storage_augment" scale="0.5" /> <Color id="aqua">Stabilized RF Coil</Color> | Greatly increases energy storage capacity |
| <ItemImage id="thermal:rf_coil_xfer_augment" scale="0.5" /> <Color id="aqua">Twin-Flux RF Coil</Color> | Greatly increases energy transfer speed |

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

***

<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  ## <Color id="gold">Dynamo Augments</Color>
</Column>

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

Augments specifically for [Dynamos](dynamos.md) to improve power generation.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

| Augment | Effect |
|---|---|
| <ItemImage id="thermal:dynamo_output_augment" scale="0.5" /> <Color id="aqua">Reaction Chamber</Color> | Increases energy output per tick |
| <ItemImage id="thermal:dynamo_fuel_augment" scale="0.5" /> <Color id="aqua">Multi-Cycle Injector</Color> | Increases total energy generated per fuel item |
| <ItemImage id="thermal:dynamo_throttle_augment" scale="0.5" /> <Color id="aqua">Dynamo Throttle</Color> | Stops the Dynamo when the connected energy storage is full, preventing fuel waste |

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Color id="gold">Tip:</Color> The Dynamo Throttle is one of the most important augments. Without it, Dynamos will keep burning fuel even when energy has nowhere to go. Always install a Throttle to avoid waste.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

***

<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  ## <Color id="gold">Other Thermal Extra Augments</Color>
</Column>

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

Thermal Extra adds two augment families that have no base-Thermal equivalent:

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="thermal_extra:dragonsteel_area_radius_augment" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Radial Enhancement</Color>
</Row>

Increases the work radius of area-effect machines like the Crop Harvester and Magmatic Accumulator. Available in all five Thermal Extra material tiers (Soul Infused, Twinite, Shellite, Dragonsteel, Abyssal).

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="thermal_extra:dragonsteel_fluid_tank_augment" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Expanded Tank Construction</Color>
</Row>

Increases the internal fluid capacity of any Thermal/Thermal Extra machine that holds a fluid buffer. Available in all five tiers.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="thermal_extra:av_item_filter_augment" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Advanced Item Filter Augment</Color>
</Row>

Enables fine-grained item filtering on machine inputs and outputs — useful for SAG Mills, Pulverizers, and any machine where you want to only process specific items from a shared inventory.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>
