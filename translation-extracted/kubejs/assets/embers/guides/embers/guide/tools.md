<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  # <Color id="gold">Tools & Equipment</Color>
  <ItemImage id="clockwork_pickaxe" scale="2" />

  Embers offers a range of Ember-powered tools, weapons, and armor. All are fueled by Ember stored in portable containers, and many can be upgraded with augments for additional abilities.
</Column>

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25" />

***

<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  ## <Color id="gold">Clockwork Tools</Color>
</Column>

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<ItemGrid>
  <ItemIcon id="clockwork_pickaxe" />
  <ItemIcon id="clockwork_axe" />
  <ItemIcon id="grandhammer" />
</ItemGrid>

All three tools require accessible Ember in your inventory to be used, and they are unbreakable. They also have high base damage, allowing all of them to double as weapons. When powered with Ember they will set hit enemies on fire.

* <ItemLink id="clockwork_pickaxe" /> — Functions as both a pickaxe and a shovel.
* <ItemLink id="clockwork_axe" /> — A powerful axe for wood and combat.
* <ItemLink id="grandhammer" /> — Destroys blocks without drops. Cannot be enchanted with Fortune or Silk Touch. Useful for clearing large areas quickly.


<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25" />

<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  ## <Color id="gold">Ember Weapons</Color>
</Column>

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="blazing_ray" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Blazing Ray</Color>
</Row>

When fired, it will unleash a straight beam of pure Ember, shooting forth until it strikes a solid block or a creature. An Ember-powered ranged weapon that draws from your portable Ember supply.


<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="cinder_staff" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Cinder Staff</Color>
</Row>

The Cinder Staff channels Ember from accessible containers in your inventory, it charges up a large sphere of pure Ember in front of you. The longer it is charged, the more damaging the sphere will be. A devastating weapon for those with deep Ember reserves.

<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  ## <Color id="gold">Augments</Color>
</Column>

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

Tools and armor can be upgraded with augments through the following process:

1. Apply a <Color id="green">Motive Core</Color> to the item at the Dawnstone Anvil.
2. Use the tool or wear the armor to accumulate <Color id="aqua">Heat</Color> through normal use.
3. Level up the item at the <Color id="green">Inferno Forge</Color> to gain augment slots.
4. Attach augments at the Dawnstone Anvil.

Notable augments include:
* <Color id="green">Blasting Core</Color> — Causes explosions on use
* <Color id="green">Caster Orb</Color> — Launches projectiles
* <Color id="green">Cinder Jet</Color> — Grants a forward dash
* <Color id="green">Flame Barrier</Color> — Creates a fire shield around the wearer
* <Color id="green">Eldritch Insignia</Color> — Causes nearby mobs to flee in fear
* <Color id="green">Intelligent Apparatus</Color> — Grants bonus experience from kills

A luminescent material — place light sources at will, although it will deplete the crystal. While exposed to sunlight, the crystal will slowly regenerate.

The <ItemLink id="glimmer_lamp" /> launches light at a distance, allowing you to illuminate far-away areas without placing torches by hand.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="inflictor_gem" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Inflictor Gem</Color>
</Row>

While holding it in your hand, when taking damage, it will absorb the type of damage taken. By stitching them to your ashen armor, they will reduce the damage you take from that source by approximately a third each. Stack multiple gems of the same type for greater resistance.
