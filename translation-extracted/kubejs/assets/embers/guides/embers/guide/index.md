  # <Color id="gold">Embers Rekindled</Color>

  <ItemImage id="tinker_hammer" scale="2" />

  Embers Rekindled is a dwarven-themed technology mod built around <Color id="gold">Ember</Color> — a primal energy found deep in the earth. Mine Ember Crystals with the Ember Bore, melt metals in the Melter, alloy them in the Mixer Centrifuge, and harness Ember power through an intricate network of Emitters and Receptors. Features alchemy, augmentable tools and armor, mechanical power, and steam systems.
