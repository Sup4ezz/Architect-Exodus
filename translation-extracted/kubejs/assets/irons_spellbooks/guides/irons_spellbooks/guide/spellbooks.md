# <Color id="light_purple">Spell Books & Scrolls</Color>

<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  # <Color id="light_purple">Spell Books & Scrolls</Color>

  <ItemImage id="iron_spell_book" scale="2" />

  Spell Books hold inscribed spells for casting, while Scrolls are single-use spell items that can be inscribed into books at the Inscription Table. Higher tier books offer more spell slots and passive bonuses.
</Column>

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25" />

***

<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  ## <Color id="gold">Craftable Spell Books</Color>
</Column>

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

Each tier of spell book increases the number of spell slots available and often provides passive bonuses to your spellcasting. The crafting progression follows the standard material tiers:

<Color id="green">Copper</Color> > <Color id="white">Iron</Color> > <Color id="gold">Gold</Color> > <Color id="aqua">Diamond</Color> > <Color id="dark_red">Netherite</Color> > <Color id="light_purple">Dragonskin</Color>

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

The <Color id="light_purple">Dragonskin Spell Book</Color> is the ultimate craftable spell book. Dragonskin is obtained from the Ender Dragon, making this a true endgame item.

<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  ## <Color id="gold">Named / Unique Spell Books</Color>
</Column>

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

These rare spell books are found as drops from mobs or in special locations. They come with pre-inscribed spells that cannot be removed, but <Color id="green">CAN be upgraded</Color> at the Arcane Anvil by applying higher-level scrolls of the same spell.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="rotten_spell_book" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Rotten Spell Book</Color>
</Row>

Found in Overworld dungeons. Has 3 spell slots and comes with pre-inscribed undead-themed spells.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="druidic_spell_book" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Druidic Spell Book</Color>
</Row>

Crafted from a Rotten Spell Book combined with nature materials. Transforms the undead magic into nature-aspected spells.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="villager_spell_book" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Villager Spell Book</Color>
</Row>

Build your reputation with villagers and free captives from the Archevoker to earn this book. Contains holy and support spells.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="blaze_spell_book" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Blaze Spell Book</Color>
</Row>

A rare drop from Blazes. Contains powerful fire-school spells.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="evoker_spell_book" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Grimoire of Evokation</Color>
</Row>

Drops from Evokers and Archevokers. Contains evocation-school spells that summon fangs, vexes, and other conjurations.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="necronomicon_spell_book" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Necronomicon</Color>
</Row>

Drops from undead boss mobs. Contains powerful necromancy and blood-school spells.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  ## <Color id="gold">Casting Implements</Color>
</Column>

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

Casting implements are items that can be right-clicked to cast your active spell while providing passive benefits. They do not hold spells themselves — they cast whatever spell is active in your equipped spell book.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

- <ItemLink id="graybeard_staff" /> — Simple starter staff. A reliable casting implement for early mages.
- <ItemLink id="artificer_cane" /> — An upgraded staff with improved casting speed.
- <Color id="aqua">Ice Staff</Color> — Found in pieces scattered across the Overworld. The Cryomancer Tower may hold clues to its location and assembly.
- <Color id="dark_red">Blood Staff</Color> — Drops from undead boss mobs. Provides powerful summoning bonuses.
