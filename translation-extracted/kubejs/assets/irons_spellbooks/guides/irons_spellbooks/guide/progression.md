  # <Color id="light_purple">Progression & Crafting</Color>

  <ItemImage id="arcane_anvil" scale="2" />

  Master the crafting stations that drive your magical progression. From the Scroll Forge to the Arcane Anvil, each station unlocks new tiers of power.
</Column>

***

<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  ## <Color id="gold">Scroll Forge</Color>
</Column>

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="scroll_forge" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Crafting Scrolls</Color>
</Row>

The Scroll Forge is unlocked once you have Nether-level resources. It crafts scrolls using three components: <Color id="green">Ink</Color> (determines rarity and spell level), <Color id="green">Paper</Color>, and a <Color id="green">Focus</Color> (determines the spell school). Experiment with different ink tiers and focuses to discover new spells.


<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

***

<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  ## <Color id="gold">Ink Tiers</Color>
</Column>

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

The ink you use in the Scroll Forge determines the rarity and power level of the resulting scroll.

<ItemGrid>
  <ItemIcon id="common_ink" />
  <ItemIcon id="uncommon_ink" />
  <ItemIcon id="rare_ink" />
  <ItemIcon id="epic_ink" />
  <ItemIcon id="legendary_ink" />
</ItemGrid>

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

- <ItemLink id="common_ink" /> — Crafts the lowest level spells. Easily obtainable from basic materials.
- <ItemLink id="uncommon_ink" /> — Slightly more powerful spells. Crafted from improved materials.
- <ItemLink id="rare_ink" /> — Found in magic treasure chests or dropped by spellcasting mobs.
- <ItemLink id="epic_ink" /> — Best obtained from magic treasure or killing spellcasting mobs.
- <ItemLink id="legendary_ink" /> — The most rare ink. Crafts the most powerful spells. Some legendary spells have multiple levels that require the <Color id="green">Arcane Anvil</Color> to reach.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  ## <Color id="gold">Arcane Anvil</Color>
</Column>

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="arcane_anvil" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">The Ultimate Crafting Station</Color>
</Row>

The Arcane Anvil is the most powerful crafting station in Iron's Spells 'n Spellbooks. It requires netherite-tier resources to craft, and serves multiple purposes:

- **Level up scrolls** — Combine a scroll with higher-tier ink to increase the spell level
- **Upgrade equipment** — Apply Upgrade Orbs to armor and spell books for permanent stat boosts
- **Imbue weapons** — Apply spell scrolls to weapons, giving them magical abilities on hit


<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

***

<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  ## <Color id="gold">Upgrade Orbs</Color>
</Column>

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="upgrade_orb" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Crafting Upgrade Orbs</Color>
</Row>

Upgrade Orbs represent the final tier of progression. Craft a base <ItemLink id="upgrade_orb" />, then combine it with 8 runes of the desired attribute to create a specific upgrade (such as <ItemLink id="fire_upgrade_orb" />). Apply the finished orb to armor or spell books at the Arcane Anvil for permanent stat boosts.


<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

***

<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  ## <Color id="gold">Rare Materials</Color>
</Column>

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

Several rare materials are needed for the most powerful crafting recipes:

- <Color id="aqua">Mithril Ore</Color> — A rare Overworld ore found below y=-38 that only generates when unexposed to air. Requires a netherite pickaxe to mine.
- <Color id="red">Cinder Essence</Color> — Dropped by Ancient Knights, which are awakened by breaking armor piles found in Nether structures.
- <Color id="light_purple">Dragonskin</Color> — Dropped by the Ender Dragon. Used in the highest tier spell book and other endgame recipes.

  ## <Color id="gold">Alchemist Cauldron</Color>
</Column>

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="alchemist_cauldron" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Recycling Scrolls</Color>
</Row>

The <ItemLink id="alchemist_cauldron" /> provides a way to recycle unwanted scrolls into different, potentially more useful spells. If you find yourself with duplicate or unneeded scrolls, toss them in the cauldron for a chance at something better.
