  Magical armor empowers your spellcasting with passive bonuses. Progress from simple Wandering Magician robes through school-specific armor sets that enhance your chosen discipline.

<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  ## <Color id="gold">Armor Progression</Color>
</Column>

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="wandering_magician_helmet" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Wandering Magician</Color>
</Row>

Early game armor crafted from leather and <ItemLink id="arcane_essence" />. Provides a max mana bonus, giving you more fuel for spellcasting. A solid starting set for any aspiring mage.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="pumpkin_helmet" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Scarecrow / Pumpkin Armor</Color>
</Row>

Mid-game armor crafted from <Color id="green">Arcane Cloth</Color>. Offers improved magical stats over the Wandering Magician set without committing to a specific school.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="cryomancer_helmet" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">School Armor Sets</Color>
</Row>

Late-game armor crafted from <Color id="green">Arcane Cloth</Color> and a <Color id="green">School Rune</Color>. Each school of magic has its own armor set that empowers spells of that school. Wearing a full set provides significant bonuses to your chosen discipline.

  ## <Color id="gold">Arcane Cloth</Color>
</Column>

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="magic_cloth" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Crafting Arcane Cloth</Color>
</Row>

A mid-game crafting material made from <ItemLink id="arcane_essence" /> and other materials. Arcane Cloth is a key ingredient in both mid-tier and school-specific armor sets.


<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

***

<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  ## <Color id="gold">School Runes</Color>
</Column>

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Color id="green">Blank Runes</Color> are found in magical structures scattered across the world. School-specific mages always drop a rune of their school when defeated. Combine a Blank Rune with a school rune at a crafting table to create a school-specific rune, which is used in crafting school armor.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

***

<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  ## <Color id="gold">Upgrading Armor</Color>
</Column>

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

All armor and spell books can be upgraded at the <ItemLink id="arcane_anvil" /> using <Color id="green">Upgrade Orbs</Color>. Base Upgrade Orbs are crafted, then combined with 8 runes of the desired attribute to create a specific upgrade. Apply the upgrade to your equipment at the Arcane Anvil for permanent stat boosts.

See the [Progression & Crafting](progression.md) page for details on Upgrade Orbs and the Arcane Anvil.
