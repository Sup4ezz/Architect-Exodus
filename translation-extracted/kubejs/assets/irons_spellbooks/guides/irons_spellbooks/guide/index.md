  # <Color id="light_purple">Iron's Spells 'n Spellbooks</Color>

  <ItemImage id="arcane_essence" scale="2" />

  Iron's Spells 'n Spellbooks adds over 100 spells across 9 schools of magic. Find scrolls in dungeon loot, inscribe them into spell books at the Inscription Table, and craft new scrolls at the Scroll Forge using magical inks. Progress from basic Wandering Magician gear through school-specific armor sets, and ultimately upgrade everything at the Arcane Anvil. Explore magical structures guarded by powerful mages, defeat Ancient Knights for rare materials, and master the forbidden Eldritch school.
