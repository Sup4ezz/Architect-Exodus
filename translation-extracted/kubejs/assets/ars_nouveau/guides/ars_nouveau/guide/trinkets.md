<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  ## <Color id="gold">Curios & Trinkets</Color>
</Column>

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="amulet_of_mana_boost" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Amulet of Mana Boost / Regen</Color>
</Row>

The <ItemLink id="amulet_of_mana_boost" /> grants <Color id="green">+50 Mana</Color> to your pool. The <ItemLink id="amulet_of_mana_regen" /> increases your mana regeneration rate.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="ring_of_lesser_discount" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Rings of Discount</Color>
</Row>

The <ItemLink id="ring_of_lesser_discount" /> and <ItemLink id="ring_of_greater_discount" /> reduce spell mana costs. They also provide small bonuses to max mana and mana regeneration.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="jump_ring" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Jump Ring</Color>
</Row>

Allows you to continue jumping while airborne. Each additional jump costs mana.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="belt_of_levitation" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Belt of Levitation</Color>
</Row>

Rise into the air by sneaking while airborne. Provides slight fall damage reduction.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="belt_of_unstable_gifts" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Belt of Unstable Gifts</Color>
</Row>

Periodically grants random positive potion effects while worn.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="jar_of_light" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Jar of Light</Color>
</Row>

Summons a floating light that follows you, illuminating dark areas.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="void_jar" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Void Jar</Color>
</Row>

Automatically destroys specified items when you pick them up. Grants a small amount of mana for each item voided.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

  ## <Color id="gold">Travel & Utility</Color>
</Column>

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="warp_scroll" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Warp Scroll</Color>
</Row>

A one-time use teleport scroll. Sneak-use to save your current location, then use normally to teleport back. <Color id="yellow">Same dimension only.</Color>

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="stable_warp_scroll" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Stable Warp Scroll</Color>
</Row>

A reusable version of the Warp Scroll that also works <Color id="green">cross-dimension</Color>.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="potion_flask" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Potion Flask</Color>
</Row>

Stores up to <Color id="green">8 charges</Color> of a potion for convenient repeated use.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

### <Color id="aqua">Flask Cannons</Color>

Throws potions from your inventory as splash or lingering effects. Great for rapid potion delivery in combat.
