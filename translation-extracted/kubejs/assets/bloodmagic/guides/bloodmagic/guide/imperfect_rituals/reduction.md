# <Color id="light_purple">Reduction</Color>

<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  # <Color id="light_purple">Reduction</Color>
<ItemImage id="minecraft:quartz_block" scale="2" />

An imperfect ritual that removes the Blood Enhanced mark from an item, returning all enchantment levels to their base values.

</Column>

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

***

### <Color id="aqua">Details</Color>

* <Color id="gold">Activation Block:</Color> Quartz Block 
* <Color id="red">LP Cost:</Color> 1000
* <Color id="green">Effect:</Color> Strips the **Blood Enhanced** mark from the held item, reverting all enchantments to their original levels

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

### <Color id="aqua">Usage</Color>

1. Place a **Quartz Block** on top of the Imperfect Ritual Stone
2. Hold a **Blood Enhanced item** in your mainhand
3. Right-click the Imperfect Ritual Stone
4. The enhancement mark is removed — the stone remains for reuse

> Only works on items that have been marked by the [Enhancement](enhancement.md) ritual.
