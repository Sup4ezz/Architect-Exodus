  # <Color id="light_purple">Boundless Skies</Color>
<ItemImage id="minecraft:ancient_debris" scale="2" />

An imperfect ritual that grants temporary creative flight. This costly ritual provides 15 minutes of unrestricted flight through the heavens, sealed with a strike of lightning.

</Column>

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

***

### <Color id="aqua">Details</Color>

* <Color id="gold">Activation Block:</Color> Ancient Debris 
* <Color id="red">LP Cost:</Color> 10000
* <Color id="green">Effect:</Color> Grants creative flight for 900 seconds (15 minutes) — lightning strikes the stone on activation

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

### <Color id="aqua">Usage</Color>

1. Place **Ancient Debris** on top of the Imperfect Ritual Stone
2. Right-click the Imperfect Ritual Stone
3. Lightning strikes — flight is granted for 15 minutes
4. The stone remains for reuse and the effect can be reapplied before it expires

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

><Color id="dark_red">**Warning:**</Color> The high LP cost makes this a premium ritual. Ensure your Soul Network has sufficient reserves before activating.
