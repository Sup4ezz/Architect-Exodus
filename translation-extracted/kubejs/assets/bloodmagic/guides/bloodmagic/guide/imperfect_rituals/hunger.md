# <Color id="light_purple">Hunger</Color>

<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  # <Color id="light_purple">Hunger</Color>
<ItemImage id="minecraft:bone_block" scale="2" />

An imperfect ritual that induces extreme hunger, drastically reducing your food level and leaving you ravenous.

</Column>

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

***

### <Color id="aqua">Details</Color>

* <Color id="gold">Activation Block:</Color> Bone Block 
* <Color id="red">LP Cost:</Color> 500
* <Color id="green">Effect:</Color> Sets your food level to **1** and saturation to **10**, triggering the ravenous hunger effect

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

### <Color id="aqua">Usage</Color>

1. Place a **Bone Block** on top of the Imperfect Ritual Stone
2. Right-click the Imperfect Ritual Stone
3. Hunger is applied instantly — the stone remains for reuse

<Color id="dark_red">**Warning:**</Color> Have food ready before activating. Your food level will drop to near-zero immediately.
