***

### <Color id="aqua">Details</Color>

* <Color id="gold">Activation Block:</Color> Arcane Anvil 
* <Color id="red">LP Cost:</Color> 3500
* <Color id="green">Effect:</Color> Grants **Echoing Strikes III** for 15 minutes (Iron's Spellbooks compatibility)

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

### <Color id="aqua">Usage</Color>

1. Place an **Arcane Anvil** on top of the Imperfect Ritual Stone
2. Right-click the Imperfect Ritual Stone
3. Echoing Strikes III is applied — the stone remains for reuse
