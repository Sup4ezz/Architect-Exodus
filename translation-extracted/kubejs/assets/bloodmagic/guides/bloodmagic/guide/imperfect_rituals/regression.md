<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  # <Color id="light_purple">Regression</Color>
<ItemImage id="minecraft:bookshelf" scale="2" />

An imperfect ritual that strips away the accumulated repair penalty from tools and weapons, making expensive items repairable again.

</Column>

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

***

### <Color id="aqua">Details</Color>

* <Color id="gold">Activation Block:</Color> Bookshelf 
* <Color id="red">LP Cost:</Color> 3000
* <Color id="green">Effect:</Color> Removes the RepairCost NBT tag from the held item, resetting its anvil repair penalty to zero

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

### <Color id="aqua">Usage</Color>

1. Place a **Bookshelf** on top of the Imperfect Ritual Stone
2. Hold the item you wish to reset in your mainhand
3. Right-click the Imperfect Ritual Stone
4. The repair cost is cleared — the stone remains for reuse
