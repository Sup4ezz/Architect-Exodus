<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  # <Color id="red">Blood Magic</Color>

  <ItemImage id="bloodmagic:altar" scale="2" />

  Blood Magic is a mod centered around the use of <Color id="red">Life Points (LP)</Color> — a resource generated by sacrificing health. With LP you can craft powerful items, fuel rituals, and create Living Armor that grows stronger as you wear it.
</Column>

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25" />

***

<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  ## <Color id="gold">General Progression</Color>
</Column>

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.5"/>

1. Build a <ItemLink id="bloodmagic:altar" /> and craft a <ItemLink id="bloodmagic:sacrificialdagger" />
2. Craft <ItemLink id="bloodmagic:blankslate" /> to unlock further recipes
3. Create <ItemLink id="bloodmagic:weakbloodorb" /> to build your Soul Network
4. Upgrade your Altar Tiers with <ItemLink id="bloodmagic:blankrune" />
5. Explore Alchemy, Demon Will, and Sigils
6. Set up Rituals for powerful area effects
7. Forge <ItemLink id="bloodmagic:livingplate" /> that upgrades itself
<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.5" />
