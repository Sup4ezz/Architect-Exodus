<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  ## <Color id="gold">Farming</Color>
</Column>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="greenhouse_glass" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Greenhouse Glass</Color>
</Row>

Speeds up plant growth when placed above crops. Requires sunlight hitting the glass from above and a clear path between the glass and the plant below. A simple and effective way to boost farm output.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25" />

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="worm" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Worms</Color>
</Row>

When <ItemLink id="actuallyadditions:worm" />s are placed down on grass or dirt, they will till, hydrate, and speed up crop growth in a 3x3 area around them. They can be found by tilling grass with a hoe and removed by simply breaking the block they are on.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25" />

<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  ## <Color id="gold">Phantomfaces</Color>
</Column>

<ItemGrid>
  <ItemIcon id="phantom_liquiface" />
  <ItemIcon id="phantom_energyface" />
</ItemGrid>

Phantomfaces simulate a remote inventory, tank, or energy receiver at their location. There are Item, Fluid, and Energy variants. Connect a Phantomface to its target with the Phantom Connector — right-click the target block first, then right-click the Phantomface.

<Color id="gold">Note:</Color> Phantomfaces are not a wireless transport system. They are meant for convenience when physical space is tight, making a distant container accessible from a nearby location.

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="phantom_booster" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Phantom Booster</Color>
</Row>

The default connection range can be extended by stacking up to 3 <ItemLink id="phantom_booster" /> blocks on top of the Phantomface.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25" />

<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  ## <Color id="gold">Experience Solidifier</Color>
</Column>

Converts player XP into Solidified Experience items. Stand near it and it will drain your experience levels. It also picks up XP orbs from the ground nearby, making it useful next to mob farms.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25" />

<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  ## <Color id="gold">Coffee Machine</Color>

<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  ## <Color id="gold">Shock Absorber</Color>
</Column>

Protects an area from explosions by absorbing the blast damage. Consumes CF per explosion absorbed. Place it where you want to prevent creeper or TNT damage.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25" />

***

<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  ## <Color id="gold">Sacks</Color>
</Column>

<ItemLink id="actuallyadditions:travelers_sack" />s are not only an easy way to transport items around, but they can also be configured to automatically pick up all or specific items from the ground.

<ItemLink id="actuallyadditions:void_sack" />s have the same functionality as normal ones, however, they remove items from the world instead of storing them.
