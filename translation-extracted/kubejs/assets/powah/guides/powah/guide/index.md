# <Color id="yellow">Powah</Color>

<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  # <Color id="yellow">Powah</Color>

  <ItemImage id="energizing_orb" scale="2" />

  Powah is a complete energy system — generate FE from fuel, heat, sunlight, or nuclear reactors, store it in cells and batteries, and transfer it through cables or the wireless Ender Network. Every machine comes in 7 tiers from Starter to Nitro.
</Column>

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

***

<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  ## <Color id="gold">General Progression</Color>
</Column>

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.5"/>

1. Craft a <ItemLink id="wrench" /> — your essential tool for configuring cables and linking machines
2. Build a Starter-tier generator like the <ItemLink id="furnator_starter" /> or <ItemLink id="magmator_starter" />
3. Connect it with <ItemLink id="energy_cable_starter" /> to an <ItemLink id="energy_cell_starter" /> for storage
4. Set up an <ItemLink id="energizing_orb" /> with <ItemLink id="energizing_rod_starter" /> to craft advanced Powah components
5. Upgrade your machines through the tier system — Starter, Basic, Hardened, Blazing, Niotic, Spirited, Nitro
6. Build a <ItemLink id="reactor_starter" /> fueled by <ItemLink id="uraninite" /> for endgame power generation
