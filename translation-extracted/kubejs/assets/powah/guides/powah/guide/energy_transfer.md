  ## <Color id="gold">Ender Network</Color>
</Column>

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="ender_cell_starter" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Ender Cell</Color>
</Row>

Stores FE in a specific Ender Network channel owned by the player. Access a channel's stored energy from anywhere in the world if the Ender Cell has an active channel with valid capacity.

![](./storage_transfer/ender_cell.png)

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="ender_gate_starter" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Ender Gate</Color>
</Row>

Transfers energy between an adjacent block and the Ender Network. Unlike the Ender Cell, you cannot upgrade the network from an Ender Gate.

![](./storage_transfer/ender_gate.png)

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>
