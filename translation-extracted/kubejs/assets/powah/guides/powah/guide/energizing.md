The Orb handles the energizing process itself.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="energizing_rod_starter" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Energizing Rods</Color>
</Row>

The energizing speed depends on the number of rods and their tier. The Orb does not require energy, but the rods must be placed on cables or any FE block to work.
