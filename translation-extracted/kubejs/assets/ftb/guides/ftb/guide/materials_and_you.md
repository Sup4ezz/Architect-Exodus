  ## <Color id="gold">GeOre Geodes</Color>
</Column>

Many dimensions use the <Color id="aqua">GeOre</Color> system rather than conventional vein mining. Ores grow as regenerating clusters inside hollow geodes. Mine the cluster node to receive shards, the raw material form. The budding block at the geode's core will slowly regrow clusters if left in place.
