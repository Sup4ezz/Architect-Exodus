<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  # <Color id="aqua">Jotunheim</Color> <Color id="light_purple">& Niflheim</Color>

  <ItemImage id="occultism:iesnium_ore" scale="2" />

  The frost and mist realms are your Tier 1 mining destinations. Both unlock the full Thermal metals set and share most of their GeOre spread. Jotunheim is the entry point; Niflheim is the richer of the two and the best location for a permanent mining base.
</Column>

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

***

<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  ## <Color id="gold">Shared GeOre Geodes</Color>
</Column>

Both dimensions carry these geode types. Niflheim additionally has faster-regenerating budding variants of Nickel and Silver geodes.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="geore:coal_shard" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Coal, Copper, Iron, Quartz, Redstone</Color>
</Row>

The standard five geodes, with denser generation than Hel.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="geore:lapis_shard" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Lapis</Color>
</Row>

First available in geode form here. Needed for enchanting and several crafting paths.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="geore:gold_shard" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Gold</Color>
</Row>

Gold geodes appear for the first time. Required for mid tier crafting and alloying.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

***

<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  ## <Color id="gold">Shared Vein Ores</Color>
</Column>

Both dimensions carry the complete Thermal metals set as underground vein ores.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="ftbmaterials:tin_stone_ore" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Tin, Lead, Silver, Nickel, Apatite, Cinnabar, Sulfur & Niter</Color>
</Row>

Tin for Bronze alloys, Nickel for Invar, Silver for higher tier Thermal recipes, and Apatite/Cinnabar/Sulfur/Niter for Thermal's chemical processing chains.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

***

<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  ## <Color id="aqua">Jotunheim</Color>
</Column>

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="minecraft:diamond_ore" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Diamond</Color>
</Row>

Present in small veins (size 3). Rare here. Niflheim is a significantly better source for both.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="ae2:quartz_cluster" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Certus Quartz Geode</Color>
</Row>

AE2's Certus Quartz grows in <Color id="aqua">water filled geodes</Color> with budding quartz blocks at the core. Mine clusters for raw certus, or leave the budding block intact as it slowly regenerates clusters over time. <Color id="green">Required to start Applied Energistics 2 progression.</Color>

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="geore:quartz_shard" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Black Quartz Geode</Color>
</Row>

A Black Quartz geode variant spawns here. Black Quartz is used in Actually Additions recipes and several multi step crafting chains.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

***

<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  ## <Color id="light_purple">Niflheim</Color>
</Column>

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="minecraft:diamond_ore" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Diamond</Color>
</Row>

Generates between y -64 and -20 in solid counts. <Color id="green">Niflheim is the recommended diamond farming location.</Color> Cave and ceiling variants also exist.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="minecraft:emerald_ore" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Emerald</Color>
</Row>

Spawns between y -64 and -50. Not restricted to mountain biomes here. Emeralds can be found in any Niflheim underground. Cave and ceiling variants present.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="minecraft:gold_ore" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Gold</Color>
</Row>

Standard gold vein generation at depth, in addition to the geode form.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="occultism:iesnium_ore" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Iesnium</Color>
</Row>

The premier resource of Niflheim. Iesnium spawns in very large veins (size 12) between y -64 and -20, replacing both stone and deepslate. Required for advanced Occultism rituals, dimensional mining, and several endgame machines. Cave and ceiling variants also spawn. <Color id="green">Stock up here. Demand only increases as you progress.</Color>

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="wizards_reborn:arcanum_ore" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Arcanum Ore</Color>
</Row>

Arcanum from Wizards Reborn spawns in Niflheim. Required for arcane machinery and spell progression.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="malum:brilliant_stone" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Brilliant Ore</Color>
</Row>

A Malum ore that yields Brilliant Shards, used in soul infused crafting and the Malum magic system.
