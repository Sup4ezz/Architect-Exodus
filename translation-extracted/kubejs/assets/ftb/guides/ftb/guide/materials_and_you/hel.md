<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  # <Color id="red">Helheim</Color>

  <ItemImage id="geore:iron_shard" scale="2" />

  Helheim is built around scarcity. Ores exist but are sparse by design. Gather what you need and move on to Jotunheim or Niflheim for serious resource extraction.
</Column>

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

***

<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  ## <Color id="gold">GeOre Geodes</Color>
</Column>

All Helheim ore generation uses the GeOre geode system. Locate hollow geodes underground and mine the cluster nodes inside. Only the essential five are present.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>
