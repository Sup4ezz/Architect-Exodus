<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  # <Color id="gold">Muspelheim </Color>

  <ItemImage id="minecraft:ancient_debris" scale="2" />

  The fire realm shares its ore structure with the Nether. GeOre geodes are present, but Muspelheim's defining resource is Ancient Debris, the path to Netherite. Iesnium also spawns here in smaller quantities than Niflheim.
</Column>

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

***

<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  ## <Color id="gold">GeOre Geodes</Color>
</Column>

The standard geode spread is present, embedded in Muspelheim's netherrack.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="geore:coal_shard" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Coal, Copper, Iron, Lapis, Quartz, Redstone, Gold</Color>
</Row>

Full standard geode set. Same composition as Jotunheim, with generation adapted to Nether-type stone.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="geore:ancient_debris_shard" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Ancient Debris</Color>
</Row>

Ancient Debris also spawns in GeOre geode form here, in addition to the standard vein types. The geode clusters provide a renewable source alongside conventional strip mining.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

***

<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  ## <Color id="gold">Unique Resources</Color>
</Column>

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="minecraft:ancient_debris" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Ancient Debris</Color>
</Row>

Both the large and small Ancient Debris vein types spawn in Muspelheim. This is the primary source of <Color id="gold">Netherite Scrap</Color> and the path to Netherite tools and armor. Standard Nether mining depth rules apply. Strip mine at low y levels for best results.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="occultism:iesnium_ore" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Iesnium</Color>
</Row>

Iesnium spawns in Muspelheim but in smaller veins (size 3) than in Niflheim, replacing netherrack only. If you need large quantities, Niflheim remains the better source.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="minecraft:nether_quartz_ore" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Nether Quartz</Color>
</Row>

Spawns in <Color id="aqua">Forlorn Hollows</Color> biome only. Not distributed across all of Muspelheim. Seek out this specific biome if you need a dedicated quartz source beyond the geode form.
