  # <Color id="gold">Asgard </Color>

  <ItemImage id="geore:diamond_shard" scale="2" />

  The divine realm is sparse by design, an endgame dimension and not a mining destination. All of Asgard's ores arrive through the GeOre geode system only. No conventional vein mining, no resources specific to this dimension. Its value is in having access to the full ore table in a single location after you've progressed far enough to reach it.
</Column>


> <Color id="yellow">Asgard has no unique ores of its own.</Color> For high-volume mining, Niflheim remains the most efficient location. Asgard's ore access is a convenience for players who have made it their primary base.

***

  ## <Color id="gold">GeOre Geodes — Full Spread</Color>
