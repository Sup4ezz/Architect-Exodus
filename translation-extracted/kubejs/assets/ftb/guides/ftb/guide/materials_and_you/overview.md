  ### <Color id="aqua">Blaze Powder</Color>
</Row>

You won't have access to blazes until Muspelheim, but you can still acquire Blaze Powder early using any of these methods:

1. Centrifuging <Color id="green">Magma Cream</Color> into a <Color id="aqua">Centrifugal Separator</Color>.
2. Throwing <Color id="yellow">Glowstone</Color> blocks into liquid <Color id="red">Pyrotheum</Color> placed in the world.
3. Dehydrating <Color id="red">Lava</Color> inside an <Color id="aqua">Endothermic Dehydrator</Color>.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

  ### <Color id="aqua">Diamonds & Emeralds</Color>
  <ItemImage id="minecraft:emerald" />

You can acquire early Diamonds and Emeralds in Jotunheim and Niflheim by mining deep underground or rarely by exploring structures.
