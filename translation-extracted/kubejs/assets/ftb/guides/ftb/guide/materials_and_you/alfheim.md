<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  # <Color id="light_purple">Alfheim </Color>

  <ItemImage id="mythicbotany:elementium_ore" scale="2" />

  The light elven realm has a purely magic-themed ore set. No industrial metals, no Thermal ores, no AE2 crystals. What Alfheim offers instead are ores tied to Botania, Malum, and Occultism, the magical progression mods.
</Column>

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

***

<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  ## <Color id="gold">Standard Ores</Color>
</Column>

Vanilla ores are present in Alfheim through the dimension's biome definitions. Standard Minecraft ore generation: coal, iron, gold, redstone, diamond, lapis, copper, and emerald are all available at typical depth distributions.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

***

<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  ## <Color id="gold">Magic-Tier Ores</Color>
</Column>

These ores are unique to Alfheim and its magical character. None have industrial uses. Each feeds into a specific magic system.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="mythicbotany:elementium_ore" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Elementium Ore</Color>
</Row>

A Mythic Botany ore that yields Elementium Ingots. Required for the upper tier of Botania progression, including advanced mana generation structures.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="mythicbotany:dragonstone_ore" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Dragonstone Ore</Color>
</Row>

Another Mythic Botany ore, yielding Dragonstones used in high-end Botania equipment and altar upgrades.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="occultism:iesnium_ore" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Iesnium</Color>
</Row>

Iesnium spawns in Alfheim in small veins (size 3) between y -64 and 50. Sparse compared to Niflheim's size-12 deposits, but accessible if you're operating in Alfheim without a Niflheim base.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="malum:soulstone_ore" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Soulstone Ore</Color>
</Row>

Malum's Soulstone Ore. Soulstone is the core resource of the Malum magic system, used in soul infused rituals, altars, and enchanting. <Color id="green">Alfheim is a reliable source.</Color>

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="malum:brilliant_stone" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Brilliant Ore</Color>
</Row>

Brilliant Ore yields Brilliant Shards, used in Malum's higher-tier crafting. Malum also spawns its own Natural Quartz variant here, distinct from standard Nether Quartz.
