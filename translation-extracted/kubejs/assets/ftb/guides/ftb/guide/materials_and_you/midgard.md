<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  # <Color id="green">Midgard </Color>

  <ItemImage id="minecraft:grass_block" scale="2" />

  The overworld has the broadest ore variety of any dimension: vanilla ores, the full Thermal metals set, Create's Zinc, Mystical Agriculture seeds, and several endgame tier resources that exist nowhere else. A dimension worth returning to at every tier.
</Column>

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  ## <Color id="gold">Vanilla Ores</Color>
</Column>

All standard Minecraft ores are present at their normal y level distributions.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="minecraft:iron_ore" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Iron, Copper, Gold, Coal</Color>
</Row>

Standard overworld metals. Iron and Copper are especially important in early Midgard for the volume of crafting recipes that require them.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="minecraft:diamond_ore" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Diamond, Emerald, Lapis, Redstone</Color>
</Row>

Present at vanilla depth distributions. For serious diamond farming, Niflheim is more efficient, but Midgard's diamond ore is accessible without interdimensional travel.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

***

<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  ## <Color id="gold">Modded Vein Ores</Color>
</Column>

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="ftbmaterials:tin_stone_ore" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Tin, Lead, Silver, Nickel, Apatite, Cinnabar, Sulfur, Niter</Color>
</Row>

The full Thermal metals set also spawns in Midgard as standard vein ores, mirroring Jotunheim and Niflheim.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="create:zinc_ore" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Zinc</Color>
</Row>

Create's Zinc spawns as a standard overworld vein ore. Required for Brass (Zinc + Copper) and essential for Create machinery progression.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="mysticalagriculture:prosperity_ore" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Inferium & Prosperity Ore</Color>
</Row>

Mystical Agriculture's seed ores. Inferium Ore provides raw Inferium Essence, the foundation of the entire MA seed tier. Prosperity Ore yields Prosperity Shards for crafting higher tier seeds and tools.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="occultism:iesnium_ore" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Iesnium</Color>
</Row>

Iesnium is present in Midgard as a standard vein ore. Not as abundant as Niflheim's size-12 deposits, but accessible without dimensional travel once you've unlocked Midgard.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

***

<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  ## <Color id="gold">Unique & Endgame Resources</Color>
</Column>

Several ores exist only in Midgard, typically accessed during Tier 2 and beyond.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="draconicevolution:overworld_draconium_ore" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Draconium Ore</Color>
</Row>

Draconic Evolution's Draconium spawns in the overworld. The endgame energy and equipment mod. Draconium Ore is required to start its progression chain.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="powah:uraninite_ore" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Uraninite Ore</Color>
</Row>

Powah's Uraninite spawns in three variants: normal, dense, and poor. Used in Powah's energy generation system for late game power production.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="xycraft_world:kivi" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">XyCraft Ores</Color>
</Row>

Five colored ore variants plus Aluminum and Kivi stone. Used in XyCraft machinery and several cross mod recipes.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="iceandfire:sapphire_ore" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Sapphire Ore</Color>
</Row>

Ice and Fire's Sapphire Ore spawns in the overworld. Sapphires are used in Ice and Fire dragon equipment and several cross mod recipes.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="iceandfire:silver_ore" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Silver Ore</Color>
</Row>

Ice and Fire's Silver Ore provides an overworld silver source independent of Jotunheim's vein ores. Silver is effective against many Ice and Fire mobs.
