  Once your alchemy setup is running, you can produce advanced materials needed for the Arcane Iterator and ritual components.
</Column>

***

<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  ## <Color id="gold">Arcane Linen</Color>
</Column>

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="arcane_linen" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Arcane Linen</Color>
</Row>

Grow <ItemLink id="arcane_linen_seeds" /> to harvest Arcane Linen, a textile used in various crafting recipes and as an ingredient for Alchemy Oil.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

***

<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  ## <Color id="gold">Alchemy Oil</Color>
</Column>

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="alchemy_oil_bucket" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Alchemy Oil</Color>
</Row>

Alchemy Oil is a fluid produced through the Alchemy Machine. It requires ingredients from multiple progression paths including steam/fluid handling, <ItemLink id="arcane_linen" />, and <ItemLink id="petals" />. Alchemy Oil is used to create the various types of Calx.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

***

<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  ## <Color id="gold">Calx Types</Color>
</Column>

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

Calx materials are refined from Alchemy Oil and serve as ingredients for high-tier crafting:

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="alchemy_calx" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Alchemy Calx</Color>
</Row>

A basic calx used in advanced alchemy recipes.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="enchanted_calx" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Enchanted Calx</Color>
</Row>

A magically-infused calx for iterator recipes.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="scorched_calx" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Scorched Calx</Color>
</Row>

A heat-treated calx. Used to produce <ItemLink id="arcacite" />.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="natural_calx" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Natural Calx</Color>
</Row>

An organically-derived calx used in nature-aligned recipes.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

***

<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  ## <Color id="gold">Arcacite</Color>
</Column>

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="arcacite" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Arcacite</Color>

Arcacite is a high-tier material refined from <ItemLink id="scorched_calx" />. It is a key ingredient for the <ItemLink id="arcane_iterator" /> and other endgame crafting recipes.
