<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  ## <Color id="gold">Growing Crystals</Color>
</Column>

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

To grow crystals:

1. Place a Crystal Seed in the world
2. Apply <ItemLink id="arcanum_dust" /> to the seed
3. Wait for the seed to grow into a full crystal
4. Harvest the mature crystal

The harvested crystals are used in spellcasting, rituals, and advanced crafting recipes.

  ## <Color id="gold">Spellcasting</Color>
</Column>

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="arcane_wand" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Arcane Wand</Color>
</Row>

The Arcane Wand is your spellcasting tool. Combine the wand with a harvested crystal to attune it and begin casting spells. The type of crystal determines which spells become available.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

Spells are unlocked through the <Color id="aqua">Arcanemicon</Color> — the in-game spell book for Wizard's Reborn. Consult it to discover available spells and how to configure your wand for different elemental effects.
