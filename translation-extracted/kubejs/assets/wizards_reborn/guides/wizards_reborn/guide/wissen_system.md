  ## <Color id="gold">Wissen Generation</Color>
</Column>

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="wissen_altar" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Wissen Altar</Color>
</Row>

The Wissen Altar converts <ItemLink id="arcanum" /> into Wissen energy. Place Arcanum into the altar and it will slowly generate Wissen that can be extracted by other devices.

<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  ## <Color id="gold">Wissen Tools</Color>
</Column>

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="wissen_wand" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Wissen Wand</Color>
</Row>

The Wissen Wand is a multi-purpose tool for managing your Wissen network. <Color id="aqua">Sneak + Right Click</Color> to cycle through its modes:

- <Color id="aqua">Receive Connect Mode</Color> — Links a source to a destination. Right-click the source first, then right-click the destination. Wissen will flow from source to destination.
- <Color id="aqua">Send Connect Mode</Color> — The reverse: right-click the destination first, then right-click the source to pull Wissen.
- <Color id="aqua">Reload</Color> — Resets all connections on the targeted block.
- <Color id="aqua">Off</Color> — Disables the targeted block.
- <Color id="aqua">Function Mode</Color> — Activates the targeted block.

<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  ## <Color id="gold">Wissen Transport & Storage</Color>
</Column>

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="wissen_translator" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Wissen Translator</Color>
</Row>

Wissen Translators transfer Wissen between blocks over a distance. Each translator supports <Color id="red">one input and one output</Color> connection. Use the <ItemLink id="wissen_wand" /> to set up connections.


<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="wissen_cell" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Wissen Cell</Color>
</Row>

The Wissen Cell stores Wissen energy for later use. Connect it to your Wissen network to buffer energy between your altar and your machines.
