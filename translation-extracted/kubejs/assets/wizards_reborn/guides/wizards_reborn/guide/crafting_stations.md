<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  ## <Color id="gold">Arcane Workbench</Color>
</Column>

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="arcane_workbench" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Arcane Workbench</Color>
</Row>

The Arcane Workbench is the primary crafting station for Wizard's Reborn. It uses Wissen to craft magical items and components. Connect it to your Wissen network to power it.


<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

***

<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  ## <Color id="gold">Wissen Crystallizer</Color>
</Column>

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="wissen_crystallizer" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Wissen Crystallizer</Color>
</Row>

The Wissen Crystallizer uses Wissen to create Crystal Seeds and process crystalline materials. It is essential for progressing into elemental crystals and spellcasting.


<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

  ## <Color id="gold">Arcane Wood Mortar</Color>
</Column>

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="arcane_wood_mortar" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Arcane Wood Mortar</Color>
</Row>

The Mortar grinds items into other forms. One important use is grinding flowers into <ItemLink id="petals" />, which are needed for later alchemy recipes.
