<Row>
  <ItemImage id="light_transfer_lens" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Light Transfer Lens</Color>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="runic_pedestal" />

<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  ## <Color id="gold">Performing Rituals</Color>
</Column>

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

To perform a ritual:

1. Craft a <ItemLink id="runic_wisestone_plate" /> — this is the ritual's central block
2. Provide light to the plate using a <ItemLink id="light_emitter" /> and <ItemLink id="light_transfer_lens" /> as needed
3. Place required items on <ItemLink id="runic_pedestal" /> blocks around the plate
4. Activate the ritual

Different runic plates correspond to different rituals. The <Color id="aqua">Crystal Infusion</Color> ritual is one of the first available and allows you to infuse items with crystal energy.
