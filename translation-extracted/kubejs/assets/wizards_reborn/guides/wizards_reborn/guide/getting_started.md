***

<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  ## <Color id="gold">Arcanum Resources</Color>
</Column>

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="arcanum" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Arcanum</Color>
</Row>

Arcanum is the foundational resource of Wizard's Reborn. Mine Arcanum Ore underground to obtain raw Arcanum. Process it into <ItemLink id="arcanum_dust" /> which is used to transmute other materials and fuel various processes.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

***

<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  ## <Color id="gold">Arcane Wood</Color>
</Column>

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="arcane_wood_sapling" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Arcane Wood Sapling</Color>
</Row>

Use <ItemLink id="arcanum_dust" /> on any ordinary sapling to transmute it into an Arcane Wood Sapling. Plant and grow it to harvest Arcane Wood, which is used in many Wizard's Reborn crafting recipes.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

***

<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  ## <Color id="gold">Arcane Gold</Color>
</Column>

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="raw_arcane_gold" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Arcane Gold</Color>
</Row>

Use <ItemLink id="arcanum_dust" /> on any Gold Ore block in the world to transmute it into Arcane Gold Ore. Mine the transmuted ore to obtain Raw Arcane Gold, which can be smelted into Arcane Gold Ingots for advanced recipes.
