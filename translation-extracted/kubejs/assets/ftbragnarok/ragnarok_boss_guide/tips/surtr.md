# <Color id="light_purple">Surtr</Color> - Lord of the Fire Giants
<ItemImage id="ftbragnarok:flame_heart" scale="2" />

If you are struggling against <Color id="light_purple">Surtr</Color>, here are some upgrades you could look into:
</Column>

***

<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
### <ItemImage id="ftbragnarok:flame_heart" scale="0.75" /> <Color id="aqua">Recommended Strategy</Color> <ItemImage id="ftbragnarok:flame_heart" scale="0.75" />
</Column>

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>


### <Color id="green">Puffish Skills</Color>

Spend the Skill Points you have been given via quest rewards.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>


<Row>
  <ItemImage id="bhc:yellow_heart" />
  ### <Color id="yellow">Baubly Heart Canisters</Color>
</Row>

Trade for <Color id="yellow"><ItemLink id="bhc:yellow_heart" />s</Color> from the Bifrost Traders with <Color id="gold">Dawnstone Aspectus</Color> and <Color id="light_purple">Dilluted Power</Color>.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>


<Row>
  <ItemImage id="minecraft:netherite_chestplate" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Netherite</Color>
</Row>

Reaching the land of hell, <Color id="light_purple"><ItemLink id="minecraft:ancient_debris" /></Color> allows you to make Minecraft's best armor: <Color id="dark_purple">Netherite Armor</Color>.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>


<Row>
  <ItemImage id="cataclysm:ignitium_chestplate" />
  ### <Color id="green">Cataclysm</Color>
</Row>

Using <Color id="gold"><ItemLink id="cataclysm:ignitium_ingot"/></Color>, you can craft armor that comes with several innate buffs, including immortality, as well as very high base stats.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>


<Row>
  <ItemImage id="malum:malignant_stronghold_chestplate" />
  ### <Color id="green">Malum</Color>
</Row>

With access to <Color id="light_purple"><ItemLink id="minecraft:ancient_debris" /></Color> you can also craft <Color id="dark_purple"><ItemLink id="malum:malignant_pewter_plating"/></Color>, allowing you to upgrade your Soul Stained Steel armor, scythe, and axe. Rings, brooches, and belts with combat-focused bonuses can provide a meaningful stat boost.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>


<Row>
  <ItemImage id="reliquary:salamander_eye" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Reliquary - Salamander's Eye</Color>
</Row>

One of the most useful held items for this fight. While the <ItemLink id="reliquary:salamander_eye" /> is in your hand or offhand, it:

- <Color id="green">Automatically douses blaze fireballs</Color> including the ones from Surtr's summoned Blazes.
- <Color id="green">Reflects ghast-type fireballs</Color> in the direction you're looking.
- <Color id="green">Extinguishes fire on and around you</Color> cancelling your burning ticks.


<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.5"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="minecraft:potion" />
  ### <Color id="red">Fire Resistance Potions</Color>
</Row>

Still mandatory even with the Salamander's Eye.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="irons_spellbooks:ice_spell_book" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Ice & Frost Damage Sources</Color>
</Row>

Bring whatever cold-damage you can stack:

- <Color id="green">Iron's Spellbooks</Color> — Cone of Cold, Frostbite, Frost Spike. Cold school spells double-dip on the weakness and the cap bypass.
- <Color id="green">Ars Nouveau</Color> — Freeze / Cold Snap augmented projectile spells.
- <Color id="green">Apotheosis Frostbite enchantment</Color> on a melee weapon adds frost damage on hit.
- <Color id="green">Ice-aspected weapons</Color> from Mowzie's, Iceandfire, or any frost-themed addon — anything that registers as cold damage triggers the weakness.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.5"/>
