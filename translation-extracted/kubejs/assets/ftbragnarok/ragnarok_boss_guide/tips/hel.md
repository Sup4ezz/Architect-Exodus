### <Color id="green">Puffish Skills</Color>

Spend the Skill Points you have been given via quest rewards.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>


<Row>
  <ItemImage id="bhc:red_heart" />
  ### <Color id="red">Baubly Heart Canisters</Color>
</Row>

Farm <Color id="red"><ItemLink id="bhc:red_heart" /></Color> from the <Color id="aqua">Elemental Gateway</Color>.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>


<Row>
  <ItemImage id="malum:soul_stained_steel_chestplate" />
  ### <Color id="green">Malum</Color>
</Row>

Craft rings, necklaces, runes, brooches, and belts for extra stats and effects. <Color id="light_purple">Soustained Gear</Color> is also highly recommended as an upgrade to standard Iron Armor.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>


<Row>
  <ItemImage id="animus:imperfect_ritual_stone" />
  ### <Color id="green">Blood Magic</Color>/<Color id="green">Animus</Color>
</Row>

Tier 2 Blood Altar allows crafting of Sigils and <Color id="dark_green"><ItemLink id="animus:imperfect_ritual_stone" /></Color> granting temporary buffs and effects.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>


<Row>
  <ItemImage id="ars_nouveau:novice_spell_book" />
  ### <Color id="green">Iron's Spells</Color>/<Color id="green">Ars Nouveau</Color>
</Row>

Make new and varied spells to boost survivability and damage.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>


<Row>
  <ItemImage id="alexscaves:dreadbow" />
  ### <Color id="gold">The Forsaken</Color>
</Row>

Defeat this Mini-boss to craft new armor and the <Color id="light_purple"><ItemLink id="alexscaves:dreadbow" /></Color>, which deals massive area damage.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>
