### <ItemImage id="ftbragnarok:frost_heart" scale="0.75" /> <Color id="aqua">Recommended Upgrades</Color> <ItemImage id="ftbragnarok:frost_heart" scale="0.75" />

### <Color id="green">Puffish Skills</Color>

Spend the Skill Points you have been given via quest rewards.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>


<Row>
  <ItemImage id="bhc:red_heart" />
  ### <Color id="red">Baubly Heart Canisters</Color>
</Row>

Farm <Color id="red"><ItemLink id="bhc:red_heart" /></Color> from the <Color id="aqua">Elemental Gateway</Color>.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>


<Row>
  <ItemImage id="iceandfire:tide_blue_chestplate" />
  ### <Color id="green">Ice and Fire</Color>
</Row>

Serpents found in rivers and oceans drop their scales which can be crafted into <Color id="aqua">Tide Guardian Armor</Color>, offering diamond-tier defence as well as bonuses while touching water.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>


<Row>
  <ItemImage id="tconstruct:cobalt_ingot" />
  ### <Color id="green">TConstruct</Color>
</Row>

<Color id="blue"><ItemLink id="tconstruct:cobalt_ingot" />s </Color> obtained from <Color id="aqua">Skol</Color> yield fast, high-damage tools that can take advantage of the Fiery modifier to exploit Ymir's weakness.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>


<Row>
  <ItemImage id="malum:hallowed_gold_ingot" />
  ### <Color id="green">Malum</Color>
</Row>

With <Color id="yellow"><ItemLink id="malum:hallowed_gold_ingot"/></Color> now available, the Spirit Crucible and Spirit Altar unlock more powerful trinkets. Rings, brooches, and belts with combat-focused bonuses can provide a meaningful stat boost.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>
