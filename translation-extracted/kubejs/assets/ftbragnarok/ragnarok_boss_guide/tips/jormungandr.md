### <Color id="green">Puffish Skills</Color>

Spend the Skill Points you have been given via quest rewards.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>


<Row>
  <ItemImage id="bhc:red_heart" />
  ### <Color id="red">Baubly Heart Canisters</Color>
</Row>

Farm <Color id="red"><ItemLink id="bhc:red_heart" /></Color> from the <Color id="aqua">Elemental Gateway</Color>.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>


<Row>
  <ItemImage id="bloodmagic:livingplate" />
  ### <Color id="green">Blood Magic</Color>/<Color id="green">Animus</Color>
</Row>

Building a Tier 3 Altar unlocks access to <Color id="red">Living Armor</Color>, a scaling and upgradeable armor set. Upgrade it with <Color id="aqua">Tough Armor</Color> for slam resistance and <Color id="aqua">Sprint Assist</Color> to stay out of the Frost Aura.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>


<Row>
  <ItemImage id="malum:hallowed_gold_ingot" />
  ### <Color id="green">Malum</Color>
</Row>

With <Color id="yellow"><ItemLink id="malum:hallowed_gold_ingot"/></Color> now available, the Spirit Crucible and Spirit Altar unlock more powerful trinkets. 

By finding the <Color id="aqua">Weeping Well</Color> structure, you also gain full access to the Malum mod.

Rings, brooches, and belts with combat-focused bonuses can provide a meaningful stat boost.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>
