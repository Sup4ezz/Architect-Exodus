</Row>

Trade for <Color id="green"><ItemLink id="bhc:green_heart" />s</Color> from the Bifrost Traders with <Color id="dark_green">Terrasteel Nuggets</Color> and <Color id="light_purple">Dilluted Power</Color>.

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="mythicbotany:alfsteel_ingot" />
  ### <Color id="dark_green">Botania</Color>/<Color id="dark_green">Mythic Botany</Color>
</Row>

<Color id="green">Terrasteel</Color> armor provides passive health regeneration that helps sustain through Hati's bleed and Skoll's burning without burning through too many healing items.

<Color id="green"><ItemLink id="mythicbotany:alfsteel_ingot" /></Color> armor from MythicBotany is a strong upgrade, providing higher stats and more abilities.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25" />


<Row>
  <ItemImage id="draconicevolution:wyvern_core" />
  ### <Color id="light_purple">Draconic Evolution</Color>
</Row>

To make Wyvern gear, you'll have to have gone to <Color id="dark_purple">The End</Color>. This gear can be equipped with modules and upgraded for your needs.

The <Color id="light_purple"><ItemLink id="draconicevolution:wyvern_chestpiece"/>'s</Color> built-in energy shield absorbs a significant portion of incoming hits.

The <Color id="light_purple"><ItemLink id="draconicevolution:wyvern_sword"/></Color> and <Color id="light_purple"><ItemLink id="draconicevolution:wyvern_bow"/></Color> are the weapons made from this material.
