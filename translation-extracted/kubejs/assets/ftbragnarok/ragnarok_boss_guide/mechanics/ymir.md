<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
## <Color id="gold">Combat Behavior</Color>
</Column>

Ymir is a <Color id="light_purple">melee-focused boss</Color> with devastating close-range attacks and frost magic. He moves slowly but hits incredibly hard.

### <Color id="aqua">Phase Transitions</Color>

| Phase | Health Threshold | New Abilities |
|-------|-----------------|---------------|
| 1 | 100% - 75% | Basic melee, frost aura |
| 2 | 75% - 50% | Ice spells, ground slam |
| 3 | 50% - 25% | Blizzard, ice cage |
| 4 | Below 25% | Enraged - all attacks enhanced |

### <Color id="aqua">Frost Aura</Color>
Ymir constantly emanates a freezing aura that slows and damages nearby players. The closer you are, the stronger the effect.

***
<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
## <Color id="gold">Attacks</Color>
</Column>

### <Color id="red">Devastating Slam</Color>
Ymir slams the ground, creating a shockwave that damages and knocks back all nearby players.

### <Color id="red">Ice Spike Barrage</Color>
Summons ice spikes from the ground in a line toward his target.

### <Color id="red">Frost Breath</Color>
A cone of freezing cold that deals heavy damage and applies Slowness.

### <Color id="red">Ice Block Prison</Color>
Encases a player in ice, immobilizing them temporarily. Break free or have allies break you out!

### <Color id="red">Blizzard</Color> (Phase 3+)
Creates a massive snowstorm that obscures vision and deals constant cold damage.

### <Color id="red">Chain Grab</Color>
Ymir can pull distant players toward him using magical frost chains.

***
<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
## <Color id="gold">Enraged State</Color>
</Column>

Below 25% health, Ymir enters an <Color id="red">enraged state</Color>:
* Grows 15% larger
* Gains bonus armor and regeneration
* All attacks deal increased damage
* Frost aura becomes more deadly
