<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
## <Color id="gold">Combat Behavior</Color>
</Column>

Surtr is an <Color id="light_purple">aggressive melee boss</Color> with powerful fire magic. He combines devastating sword strikes with explosive fire spells.

### <Color id="aqua">Phase Transitions</Color>

| Phase | Health Threshold | New Abilities |
|-------|-----------------|---------------|
| 1 | 100% - 75% | Basic attacks, fireballs |
| 2 | 75% - 50% | Fire breath, burning dash |
| 3 | 50% - 25% | Volcanic eruption, summon blazes |
| 4 | Below 15% | ENRAGED - Massive power boost |

### <Color id="aqua">Critical Weakness: Cold Damage!</Color>
Surtr takes <Color id="red">DOUBLE DAMAGE</Color> from cold/ice attacks, and cold damage <Color id="red">bypasses his damage cap entirely!</Color> Ice spells and frost weapons are extremely effective.

***
<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
## <Color id="gold">Attacks</Color>
</Column>

### <Color id="red">Flaming Sword Strikes</Color>
Devastating melee combos that set targets on fire.

### <Color id="red">Fireball Barrage</Color>
Launches multiple fireballs at ranged targets. Fast projectiles that explode on impact.

### <Color id="red">Fire Breath</Color>
A cone of flames in front of Surtr. Deals massive damage - stay behind him!

### <Color id="red">Burning Dash</Color>
Surtr charges toward a target, leaving a trail of fire. Used to close gaps.

### <Color id="red">Heat Surge</Color>
An expanding ring of fire that damages all nearby players.

### <Color id="red">Summon Blazes</Color>
Calls forth 2-4 Blazes to aid him in combat. Kill them to reduce incoming damage.

### <Color id="red">Inferno Armor</Color>
Surtr wraps himself in protective flames:
* <Color id="red">Reflects projectiles</Color> back at attackers (except cold projectiles!)
* Sets melee attackers on fire
* Removes Fire Resistance from nearby players

***
<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
## <Color id="gold">Enraged State</Color> (Below 15% HP)
</Column>

When Surtr drops below 15% health, he enters a terrifying <Color id="red">enraged state</Color>:
* Grows 15% larger
* Gains significant armor and armor toughness
* Gains regeneration
* Fire damage now deals <Color id="red">bonus magic damage</Color>
* Immediately summons a ring of Blazes
