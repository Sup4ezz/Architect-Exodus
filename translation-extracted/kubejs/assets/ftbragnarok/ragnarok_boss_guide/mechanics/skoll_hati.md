<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
## <Color id="gold">Dual Boss Mechanics</Color>
</Column>

This is a <Color id="light_purple">twin boss fight</Color> - both wolves must be defeated, and they work together!

### <Color id="aqua">Phase Transitions</Color>

The fight has unique phases based on <Color id="gold">combined health</Color>:

| Phase | Combined Health | Changes |
|-------|----------------|---------|
| 1 | 100% - 60% | Basic attacks, separate |
| 2 | 60% - 30% | Coordinated attacks, combos |
| 3 | Below 30% | Desperate fury, ultimate abilities |

### <Color id="aqua">The Core Mechanic: Balance</Color>
* Killing one wolf <Color id="red">enrages</Color> the other
* The surviving wolf gains massive buffs
* Best strategy: <Color id="green">damage them evenly</Color> and kill them close together

### <Color id="aqua">Wolf Roles</Color>

| Wolf | Element | Role |
|------|---------|------|
| <Color id="gold">Skoll</Color> | Fire/Sun | Aggressive attacker, fire magic |
| <Color id="light_purple">Hati</Color> | Shadow/Moon | Tactical support, darkness magic |

***
<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
## <Color id="gold">Skoll's Attacks</Color> (Fire Wolf)
</Column>

### <Color id="red">Fire Dash</Color>
Charges at a target wreathed in flames, leaving fire in its wake.

### <Color id="red">Solar Flare</Color>
A burst of fire damage in a radius around Skoll.

### <Color id="red">Blazing Bite</Color>
Powerful melee attack that inflicts burning.

### <Color id="red">Echoing Strikes</Color>
Attacks hit multiple times in quick succession.

***
<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
## <Color id="gold">Hati's Attacks</Color> (Shadow Wolf)
</Column>

### <Color id="red">Shadow Step</Color>
Teleports behind a target for a surprise attack.

### <Color id="red">Darkness Shroud</Color>
Creates an area of darkness that blinds players.

### <Color id="red">Blood Needles</Color>
Ranged attack that deals bleed damage over time.

### <Color id="red">Abyssal Shroud</Color>
Hati becomes semi-invisible and harder to hit.

***
<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
## <Color id="gold">Combined Attacks</Color>
</Column>

When the wolves coordinate:

### <Color id="red">Eclipse Strike</Color>
Both wolves attack the same target simultaneously from opposite sides.

### <Color id="red">Day and Night</Color>
Skoll creates fire zones while Hati creates shadow zones - standing in either is dangerous!

### <Color id="red">Pack Hunt</Color>
Both wolves circle and attack a single target relentlessly.

***
<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
## <Color id="gold">Enrage Mechanics</Color>
</Column>

If one wolf dies while the other lives:
* Surviving wolf <Color id="red">fully heals</Color>
* Gains <Color id="red">50% damage increase</Color>
* Gains <Color id="red">damage resistance</Color>
* Becomes much more aggressive
* New devastating abilities unlock
