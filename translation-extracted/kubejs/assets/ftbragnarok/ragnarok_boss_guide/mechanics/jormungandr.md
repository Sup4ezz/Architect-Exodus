<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
## <Color id="gold">Арена</Color>
</Column>

Jormungandr сражается на <Color id="light_purple">замёрзшем озере</Color>. Лёд служит ареной, но будьте осторожны: змей способен проломить его.

### <Color id="aqua">Механика льда</Color>
* Змей плавает под замёрзшей поверхностью
* Атаки могут <Color id="red">проламывать лёд</Color>, создавая опасные провалы
* Упав в воду, вы окажетесь во владениях змея
* Лёд постепенно восстанавливается

<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
## <Color id="gold">Атаки</Color>
</Column>

### <Color id="red">Breach Attack</Color>
The serpent launches itself through the ice, dealing massive damage to anyone caught in the impact zone. <Color id="red">Breaks ice in a large radius!</Color>

### <Color id="red">Venom Spit</Color>
Ranged poison projectiles that leave lingering venom pools on the ice.

### <Color id="red">Tail Sweep</Color>
A massive tail swipe that covers a wide arc - jump or get knocked into the water!

### <Color id="red">Whirlpool</Color>
Creates a vortex in the water that pulls players toward holes in the ice.

### <Color id="red">Constrict</Color> (Phase 3+)
The serpent wraps around a player, dealing crushing damage over time. Allies must deal damage to free them!

### <Color id="red">Tidal Wave</Color>
Summons a wave that sweeps across the arena, pushing players and breaking more ice.

***
<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
## <Color id="gold">Water Danger</Color>
</Column>

If you fall into the water:
* Jormungandr deals <Color id="red">bonus damage</Color> to players in the water
* You'll take constant <Color id="red">drowning pressure</Color>
* The serpent moves <Color id="red">much faster</Color> in its home element
* Climb out immediately!
