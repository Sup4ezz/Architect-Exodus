# <Color id="green">Generating Flora</Color>

<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  # <Color id="green">Generating Flora</Color>
  <ItemImage id="endoflame" scale="2" />
  Generating Flora produce Mana from various resources. Place them near a Mana Spreader to collect the Mana they produce.
</Column>

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

***

<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  ## <Color id="gold">Starter Generators</Color>
</Column>

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="endoflame" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Endoflame</Color>
</Row>

Burns furnace fuels dropped nearby. The starter generator — simple and reliable. Drop coal or charcoal near it to begin producing Mana.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="hydroangeas" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Hydroangeas</Color>
</Row>

Absorbs water source blocks nearby for a small amount of Mana. Very slow, meant as early passive generation. Decays after a set time.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

***

<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  ## <Color id="gold">Resource Consumers</Color>
</Column>

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="thermalily" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Thermalily</Color>
</Row>

Absorbs nearby lava source blocks for a large burst of Mana. Has a long cooldown between absorptions.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="gourmaryllis" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Gourmaryllis</Color>
</Row>

Eats food items dropped nearby. Alternating different foods yields more Mana than repeating the same food.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="munchdew" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Munchdew</Color>
</Row>

Eats leaves above it for Mana. Enters a long cooldown after eating too many.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="entropinnyum" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Entropinnyum</Color>
</Row>

Absorbs TNT explosions for massive Mana. Only one TNT per cycle — time your detonations carefully.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="spectrolus" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Spectrolus</Color>
</Row>

Eats wool in a specific color sequence (all 16 colors in order) for Mana. Feeding the wrong color wastes it.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="kekimurus" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Kekimurus</Color>
</Row>

Eats nearby Cake blocks for Mana. Each slice consumed generates a burst of Mana.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

***

<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  ## <Color id="gold">Mob-Based Generators</Color>
</Column>

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="rosa_arcana" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Rosa Arcana</Color>
</Row>

Converts XP orbs dropped nearby into Mana.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="narslimmus" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Narslimmus</Color>
</Row>

Kills slimes near it for Mana. More Mana from larger slimes.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="shulk_me_not" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Shulk Me Not</Color>
</Row>

Kills Shulkers near it for significant Mana. One of the highest Mana-per-kill generators.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

***

<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  ## <Color id="gold">Advanced Generators</Color>
</Column>

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="rafflowsia" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Rafflowsia</Color>
</Row>

Consumes Functional or Generating flowers dropped on it for Mana. More Mana is produced for varied flowers — avoid repeating the same type.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="dandelifeon" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Dandelifeon</Color>
</Row>

Runs a Game of Life cellular automaton on a board of cells. Complex to set up but one of the most powerful generators in Botania.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

***

<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  ## <Color id="gold">Mythic Botany Generators</Color>
</Column>

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

Mythic Botany adds several powerful generating flowers. All have floating variants.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="mythicbotany:exoblaze" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Exoblaze</Color>
</Row>

Generates Mana from Blaze powder and related fire items. A high-output generating flower for fire-themed setups.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="mythicbotany:hellebore" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Hellebore</Color>
</Row>

Generates Mana from potion effects applied to it. Throw splash potions near it to fuel it.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="mythicbotany:raindeletia" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Raindeletia</Color>
</Row>

Generates Mana from rain. Free passive generation when it's raining — perfect for areas with frequent weather.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="mythicbotany:wither_aconite" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Wither Aconite</Color>
</Row>

Generates massive amounts of Mana by consuming Nether Stars. One of the most powerful generators in the game.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>
