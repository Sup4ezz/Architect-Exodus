# <Color id="green">Functional Flora</Color>

<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  # <Color id="green">Functional Flora</Color>
  <ItemImage id="hopperhock" scale="2" />
  Functional Flora use Mana to perform various tasks. They must be near a Mana Pool to draw power.
</Column>

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

***

<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  ## <Color id="gold">Automation Flowers</Color>
</Column>

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="hopperhock" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Hopperhock</Color>
</Row>

Picks up items in range and places them in nearby inventories. The most important automation flower in Botania.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="rannuncarpus" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Rannuncarpus</Color>
</Row>

Places blocks from nearby inventories into the world. Useful for crop farms and automated building.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="clayconia" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Clayconia</Color>
</Row>

Converts sand and gravel nearby into clay blocks.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="orechid" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Orechid</Color>
</Row>

Converts stone into random ores. Very Mana-hungry but provides renewable ores.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="marimorphosis" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Marimorphosis</Color>
</Row>

Converts stone in range into various decorative stone types depending on the biome.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  ## <Color id="gold">Mythic Botany Flora</Color>
</Column>

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

Mythic Botany adds several functional flowers. All have floating variants.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="mythicbotany:aquapanthus" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Aquapanthus</Color>
</Row>

Creates water source blocks around it. A functional flower for infinite water generation.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="mythicbotany:feysythia" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Feysythia</Color>
</Row>

Converts nearby mob effects into Mana. Useful alongside mob farms that produce potion-effect mobs.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="mythicbotany:petrunia" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Petrunia</Color>
</Row>

Applies stone-related effects to nearby entities. Can petrify mobs in the area.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>
