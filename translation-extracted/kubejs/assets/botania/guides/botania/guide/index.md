  # <Color id="green">Botania</Color>

  <ItemImage id="lexicon" scale="2" />

  Botania is a nature-themed magic-tech mod where all power comes from <Color id="green">Mana</Color> generated by magical flowers. Craft flowers at the Petal Apothecary, generate Mana with Generating Flora, use it to power Functional Flora for automation, craft runes at the Runic Altar, and eventually open a portal to Alfheim for advanced recipes. Despite its floral aesthetic, Botania is a tech mod at heart — everything can be automated with redstone and ingenuity.
