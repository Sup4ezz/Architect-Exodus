<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  # <Color id="green">Инструменты и аксессуары</Color>
  <ItemImage id="terra_sword" scale="2" />
  Botania предлагает инструменты, оружие, броню и аксессуары, работающие на Mana: от базового снаряжения из Manasteel до мощной экипировки из Terrasteel и специализированного оружия.
</Column>

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

***

<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  ## <Color id="gold">Manasteel Equipment</Color>
</Column>

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

Manasteel is created by tossing Iron Ingots into a Mana Pool. Manasteel tools and armor are equivalent to Diamond tier but have the unique ability to self-repair using Mana from a <ItemLink id="mana_ring" /> or Mana Tablet in your inventory.

<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  ## <Color id="gold">Terrasteel Equipment</Color>
</Column>

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

Created at the <ItemLink id="terra_plate" /> using massive amounts of Mana. Terrasteel armor is significantly more powerful than Manasteel and provides set bonuses when worn together.

<ItemGrid>
  <ItemIcon id="terrasteel_helmet" />
  <ItemIcon id="terrasteel_chestplate" />
  <ItemIcon id="terrasteel_leggings" />
  <ItemIcon id="terrasteel_boots" />
</ItemGrid>

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="terra_sword" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Terra Blade</Color>
</Row>

Fires Mana projectiles when swung at full health. A powerful melee weapon that also works at range.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="terra_pick" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Terra Shatterer</Color>
</Row>

A pickaxe with a large area-of-effect that increases as it levels up from mining. Consumes Mana from your inventory to rank up through tiers.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

***

<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  ## <Color id="gold">Alfsteel Equipment</Color>
</Column>

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

Alfsteel tools and armor are a direct upgrade to Terrasteel and Elementium gear, crafted from <ItemLink id="mythicbotany:alfsteel_ingot" />. They have higher durability, damage, and protection values.

<ItemGrid>
  <ItemIcon id="mythicbotany:alfsteel_helmet" />
  <ItemIcon id="mythicbotany:alfsteel_chestplate" />
  <ItemIcon id="mythicbotany:alfsteel_leggings" />
  <ItemIcon id="mythicbotany:alfsteel_boots" />
  <ItemIcon id="mythicbotany:alfsteel_sword" />
  <ItemIcon id="mythicbotany:alfsteel_pick" />
  <ItemIcon id="mythicbotany:alfsteel_axe" />
</ItemGrid>

All Alfsteel equipment is crafted using the <ItemLink id="mythicbotany:alfsteel_template" /> smithing template at a Smithing Table, similar to Netherite upgrades.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="mythicbotany:mjoellnir" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Mjoellnir</Color>
</Row>

Thor's legendary hammer. An incredibly powerful weapon that can be thrown and returns to the wielder. One of the strongest weapons available through Botania progression.

You'll need to have <Color id="green">Absorption</Color> or an equipped <Color id="green">Ring of Thor</Color> to pick up this hammer.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  ## <Color id="gold">Baubles & Trinkets</Color>
</Column>

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="mana_ring" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Mana Ring / Greater Mana Ring</Color>
</Row>

Stores Mana on your person. Tools and armor in your inventory will draw from the ring to repair themselves. The Greater variant holds significantly more.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="aura_ring" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Aura Ring / Greater Aura Ring</Color>
</Row>

Passively generates Mana while worn. The Greater variant generates more. Pair with a Mana Ring to keep your tools topped off.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="travel_belt" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Soujourner's Sash</Color>
</Row>

Grants a speed boost and jump boost while worn.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="super_travel_belt" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Globetrotter's Sash</Color>
</Row>

Provides a speed boost, step assist, and jump boost all in one bauble. One of the most universally useful Botania accessories.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="knockback_belt" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Knockback Belt</Color>
</Row>

Provides knockback resistance, keeping you firmly planted when hit.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="speed_up_belt" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Speed-Up Belt</Color>
</Row>

Increases movement speed while worn.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="magnet_ring" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Magnet Ring</Color>
</Row>

Attracts nearby items and experience orbs toward you. Can be toggled off by sneaking.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="bauble_box" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Bauble Box</Color>
</Row>

A portable storage container specifically for baubles. Keeps your inventory tidy when swapping between accessories.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>
