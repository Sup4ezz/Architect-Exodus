  # <Color id="red">Chaos Guardian</Color>
  <ItemImage id="chaos_shard" scale="2" />

  Draconic Evolution's own boss, and the source of every Chaos Shard in the pack. The quest book calls it the <Color id="light_purple">Chaos Dragon</Color> and lists it under <Color id="gold">One Final Challenge</Color>, an <Color id="green">optional</Color> fight that sits past the end of the main storyline.
</Column>

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25" />

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25" />

***

<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  ## <Color id="gold">You Do Not Need Draconic Gear</Color>
</Column>

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

The vanilla assumption is that you fight this in Chaotic tier armour, but Architect's Exodus hands you plenty of gear that does the job. <Color id="green">Any endgame kit in the pack can take the guardian</Color> as long as it covers three things:

* <Color id="aqua">Sustained damage</Color>, ideally fast hits rather than one big swing. The guardian's shield has no rate limit, so attack speed and rapid-fire beat raw hit damage
* <Color id="aqua">Survivability</Color> against heavy projectile and area damage
* <Color id="aqua">Reliable flight</Color>, since the crystals hang 40 blocks in the air

Apotheosis-affixed weapons with a good gem setup, an Iron's Spellbooks build, Ice and Fire dragonsteel, Gaia tier Botania gear, a well modified Tinkers weapon or Blood Magic's living armour all clear that bar. Bring what you have already built rather than grinding a tier you do not own.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Color id="gold">What Draconic gear actually buys you</Color> is the crystal shortcut described below, plus a large shield buffer. Without it the fight is longer and needs more care, not impossible. Worth knowing: the DE chestpieces stay locked until <Color id="gold">Restored Asgard</Color>, so anyone attempting this before the end of the story is doing it in other armour by definition.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

***

<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  ## <Color id="gold">Finding the Arena</Color>
</Column>

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

Chaos Islands generate out in the End, on a grid roughly <Color id="aqua">10,000 blocks</Color> apart, so expect a long flight past the main island. Each one is about 160 blocks across with the <ItemLink id="chaos_shard" /> at its centre around <Color id="aqua">Y 80</Color>.

The guardian is already there when the island generates. Approaching the crystal wakes it.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

***

<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  ## <Color id="gold">How the Fight Works</Color>
</Column>

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

### <Color id="aqua">1. The Guardian Crystals</Color>

Fourteen crystals circle the arena, <Color id="aqua">90 blocks</Color> out from the centre and <Color id="aqua">40 blocks</Color> up. While any of them live, the guardian is effectively untouchable, so they come first.

Crystals sit behind their own shield and take only <Color id="red">a tenth</Color> of your damage while that shield holds. The shield destabilises when <Color id="light_purple">the guardian's own attacks</Color> strike a crystal, which opens a window of about <Color id="aqua">10 seconds</Color> where the crystal takes full damage. Bait the guardian into hitting its crystals, then burn them down in that window.

<Color id="green">Shortcut:</Color> damage from <Color id="red">Chaotic tier</Color> weapons destabilises a crystal on its own, no baiting required. That is the usual way people farm repeat kills, and it is also why the fight is much cheaper once you already own Chaotic gear.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

### <Color id="aqua">2. The Shield</Color>

With the crystals gone the guardian drops its own shield bar, worth <Color id="aqua">16,000</Color> points. There is no rate limit on it, so a fast weapon or a rapid-fire bow chews through it much quicker than raw damage numbers suggest.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

### <Color id="aqua">3. The Health Bar</Color>

Underneath the shield the guardian has <Color id="aqua">1,000</Color> health. This is the short part of the fight.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  ## <Color id="gold">Pack Changes to the Fight</Color>
</Column>

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Color id="light_purple">Architect's Exodus changes how the guardian handles you, not how hard it hits.</Color> In the unmodified mod its <Color id="aqua">Ground Effect</Color> and <Color id="aqua">Aerial Bombard</Color> attacks do two things once they charge past the halfway point:

* they force your <Color id="red">flight off</Color>, cancelling creative-style flight and elytra alike and dropping you out of the sky
* they <Color id="red">trap you in the arena</Color>, teleporting you back to where you were standing if you try to leave

<Color id="green">Both are switched off here.</Color> Your flight is left alone for the whole fight, nothing drags at your movement, and you can leave and come back whenever you like.

Everything else is stock: the same phases, the same projectiles, the same shield and crystal values, the same damage. The fight is not easier, it just stops taking your controls away.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Color id="yellow">One consequence.</Color> The trap also used to catch you when you fell below the arena, teleporting you back before you hit the void. That safety net is gone with it, so the drop around the island is genuinely lethal now. Bring a way to save yourself.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

***

<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  ## <Color id="gold">The Reward</Color>
</Column>

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Color id="yellow">The guardian itself drops nothing.</Color> The prize is the <ItemLink id="chaos_shard" /> it was guarding: break it once the fight is over for <Color id="gold">5 <ItemLink id="chaos_shard" /></Color>.

That is the entire supply of Chaotic tier material on that island, so any further Chaotic gear means finding another Chaos Island and doing it again.
