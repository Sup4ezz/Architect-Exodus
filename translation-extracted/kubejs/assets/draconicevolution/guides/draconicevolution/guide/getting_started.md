# <Color id="red">Getting Started</Color>

<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  # <Color id="red">Getting Started</Color>
  <ItemImage id="draconium_ingot" scale="2" />

  Draconic Evolution is a powerful endgame mod centered around Draconium, the Ender Dragon, and the Chaos Guardian. This page covers the essential materials and progression tiers you need to know.
</Column>

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25" />

***

<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  ## <Color id="gold">Draconium</Color>
</Column>

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="overworld_draconium_ore" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Draconium Ore</Color>
</Row>

In Architect's Exodus, Draconium Ore generates in <Color id="green">Muspelheim</Color>, <Color id="green">Midgard</Color>, and the <Color id="green">End</Color>. It is significantly more common in the End, so early supplies will be limited until you reach that dimension. Expect your first reliable Draconium income once you have access to Muspelheim or Midgard.

Smelt or process the ore into <ItemLink id="draconium_ingot" />, the base material for everything in the mod. <ItemLink id="draconium_dust" /> is the crushed form, useful for certain recipes and processing chains.

You'll want to start by crafting a Draconium Core.


<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25" />

***

<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  ## <Color id="gold">Tier System</Color>
</Column>

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

The mod has three tiers that determine the power level of items and equipment:

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="draconium_core" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Wyvern Tier</Color>
</Row>

The base tier. Uses <ItemLink id="draconium_core" />, crafted from Draconium Ingots. This is where all Draconic Evolution gear begins.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="awakened_core" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Draconic Tier</Color>
</Row>

The mid tier. Uses Draconic Cores (<ItemLink id="awakened_core" />), which require <ItemLink id="awakened_draconium_ingot" />. Awakened Draconium Ingots are created through Fusion Crafting using <ItemLink id="dragon_heart" />.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="chaotic_core" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Chaotic Tier</Color>
</Row>

The endgame tier. Uses <ItemLink id="chaotic_core" />, which require <ItemLink id="chaos_shard" /> obtained from the Chaos Guardian. This is the most powerful tier in the mod.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25" />

***

<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  ## <Color id="gold">Boss Drops</Color>
</Column>

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="dragon_heart" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Dragon Heart</Color>
</Row>

Dropped by the Ender Dragon upon defeat. The Dragon Heart is essential for crafting Draconic tier items. <ItemLink id="awakened_draconium_ingot" /> are created through Fusion Crafting using Dragon Hearts, unlocking the mid-tier progression path.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="chaos_shard" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Chaos Shard</Color>
</Row>

Dropped by the Chaos Guardian, the ultimate boss of Draconic Evolution. Found in the outer End near Chaos Islands, the Chaos Guardian drops <ItemLink id="chaos_shard" /> needed for Chaotic tier crafting. It is far more powerful than the Ender Dragon — come prepared with Draconic tier gear at minimum.
