  # <Color id="red">Draconic Evolution</Color>

  <ItemImage id="dragon_heart" scale="2" />

  Draconic Evolution is an endgame technology mod centered around Draconium — a powerful material obtained from dragon-slaying. It features tiered modular tools and armor (Wyvern, Draconic, Chaotic), massive Energy Cores for RF storage, Fusion Crafting, a Draconic Reactor for near-infinite power generation, teleportation via Dislocators, and the ultimate challenge: the Chaos Guardian.
