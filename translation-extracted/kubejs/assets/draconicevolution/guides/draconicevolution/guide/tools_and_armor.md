<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  ## <Color id="gold">Modular System</Color>
</Column>

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

All DE tools and armor feature a modular upgrade system. Open the <Color id="aqua">Module Configuration GUI</Color> (default keybind) to install upgrade modules into a grid system. Modules provide stat boosts and special abilities. Higher tier items have larger module grids, allowing for more powerful configurations.

<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  ## <Color id="gold">Key Modules</Color>
</Column>

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

Modules are installed into the module grid of tools and armor to customize their capabilities. Some key modules include:

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25" />

* <Color id="aqua">Energy Modules</Color> — Increase internal energy storage capacity
* <Color id="aqua">Damage / AOE Modules</Color> — Increase weapon damage and attack area
* <Color id="aqua">Speed Modules</Color> — Increase tool mining speed and movement speed
* <Color id="aqua">Shield Capacity</Color> — Increase shield hit points
* <Color id="aqua">Shield Recovery</Color> — Increase shield regeneration rate
* <Color id="aqua">Shield Control</Color> — Fine-tune shield behavior
* <Color id="green">Flight Module</Color> — Grants creative flight (requires <Color id="light_purple">Draconic</Color> tier or higher armor)
* <Color id="green">Undying Module</Color> — Prevents death at the cost of large energy consumption
* <Color id="green">Energy Link</Color> — Wirelessly draws energy from your Energy Core network
* <Color id="green">Deforestation Module</Color> — Chops entire trees in a single swing
