<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  ## <Color id="gold">Развитие полок</Color>
</Column>

<Color id="gold">Levels 1-30:</Color> Обычные книжные полки. Дополните их <Color id="aqua">свечами</Color> для Arcana, <Color id="aqua">черепами</Color> для Quanta и <Color id="aqua">скоплениями аметиста</Color> для Rectification.

<Color id="gold">Levels 30-45:</Color> Hellshelves (из материалов Нижнего мира) или Seashelves (из океанических материалов).

  <ItemImage id="hellshelf" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Hellshelf</Color>
</Row>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="seashelf" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Seashelf</Color>
</Row>

Первые улучшения по сравнению с ванильными полками. Каждый блок даёт больше Eterna, чем обычная книжная полка.


<Color id="gold">Levels 45-60:</Color> Infused Hellshelves and Infused Seashelves, created via Infusion Enchanting. Sightshelves provide Clues. Rectifiers boost Rectification.

<Color id="gold">Levels 60-75:</Color> Dormant Deepshelves, upgraded to Deepshelves, then to Echoing or Soul-touched variants using Deep Dark materials.

<Color id="gold">Levels 75-80:</Color> Sculkshelves, crafted from Warden drops.

<Color id="gold">Levels 80-90:</Color> Endshelves, requiring Infused Dragon's Breath.

<Color id="gold">Levels 90-100:</Color> Draconic Endshelf, the ultimate shelf, crafted using a Dragon Head.

<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  ## <Color id="gold">Utility Shelves</Color>
</Column>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="filtering_shelf" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Filtering Shelf</Color>
</Row>

Insert up to 6 enchanted books to blacklist those enchantments from appearing at the enchanting table. Essential for targeting specific enchantments.


<Row>
  <ItemImage id="treasure_shelf" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Treasure Shelf</Color>
</Row>

Enables treasure enchantments (including curses) to appear at the enchanting table. Normally these are loot-only, but the Treasure Shelf unlocks them for direct enchanting.
