<Row>

  Apotheosis adds dangerous Bosses to the world in several forms:
</Row>

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.5" />

- <Color id="red">Boss Dungeons</Color> — Found underground. Contain spawners, loot chests, and a powerful Boss mob.
- <Color id="gold">Invading Bosses</Color> — Spawn on the surface with a beacon beam, a sound cue, and a chat message.
- <Color id="aqua">Minibosses</Color> — Named mobs that appear randomly in the world.

Every Boss drops a guaranteed Affix item. Use a <ItemLink id="boss_summoner" /> to summon a Boss on demand.
